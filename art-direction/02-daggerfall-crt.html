II. Theme, Type, Color, Texture

MOOD STATEMENT

WITCH HUNTER IS A DELIBERATE WORLD. THE PLAYER READS SKY, ROAD, AND SILENCE BEFORE READING HEALTH. THE CRT FACE OF PAGE B MAKES READING ITSELF PART OF THE FICTION: EVERY NUMBER IS A COUNTER ON A CHURCH-CALIBRATED MACHINE, EVERY PANEL A SCRIBE'S LEDGER RENDERED IN PHOSPHOR. WARM AND WATCHFUL, NEVER NEON. THE SCREEN GLOWS LIKE A LANTERN IN A DARK ROOM, NOT LIKE A NIGHTCLUB.

TYPOGRAPHY DIRECTION

- INTERFACE TEXT: BLOCKY MONOSPACE-ADJACENT SYSTEM STACK (COURIER / FIXED-WIDTH CLASS), NEARLY ALWAYS UPPERCASE, WIDE LETTER-SPACING (2-6PX), NO ANTI-ALIAS ILLUSION - THIN STROKES LOOK BITMAP-CRUSHED.
- HEADLINES: 20-30PX, LETTER-SPACING 4-6PX, AMBER WITH A SOFT PHOSPHOR BLOOM (TEXT-SHADOW), NEVER GLOWING PULSING.
- BODY/LEDGER TEXT: 11-13PX MIXED CASE ONLY IN BOOK-AND-JOURNAL CONTEXTS (THE JOURNAL IS THE ONE DOCUMENT, THE REST IS THE MACHINE).
- NUMBERS ARE PART OF THE TYPE: TABULAR COUNTERS, ZERO-PADDED (07, 09, 12).
- NO SERIF DISPLAY, NO SANS, NO ROUNDED FONTS ANYWHERE IN UI.

RULE: AMBER IS THE MACHINE'S VOICE. PHOSPHOR GREEN APPEARS ONLY FOR MERCY-CHURCH AND HEALING CONTEXTS. RED IS DAMAGE, CURSES, DARK-COURT SEAL. VIOLET IS THE VEIL ITSELF AND IS RATIONED - IF VIOLET APPEARS, SOMETHING IS WRONG WITH THE WORLD, NOT THE UI.

TEXTURE AND LIGHTING LANGUAGE

- EVERY GRADIENT IS DITHERED OR BANDED: SKIES, PORTRAIT BACKINGS, PANEL WASHES NEVER SHOW SMOOTH FALL-OFF. 2X2 AND 4X4 CHECKER PATTERNS ARE THE DEFAULT DITHER.
- 3PX SCANLINE PASS OVER ALL FULL-SCREEN SURFACES; 1PX DARK LINE EVERY 8PX ON PANELS.
- LIGHTING READS AS PHOSPHOR DECAY: BRIGHT ELEMENTS BLOOM SLIGHTLY AND FADE AT EDGES, BUT THE WORLD BEHIND THE PANEL STAYS BLACK. THE UI NEVER BRIGHTENS THE SCENE.
- BEVELS ARE DOUBLE: OUTER 3PX METAL BORDER, INNER 4PX SHADOW GROOVE, ALL AT 90-DEGREE CORNERS.
- ICONS ARE 32-40PX STROKE GLYPHS DRAWN LIKE ENGRAVED PLATES, ONE HOBBNAIL TEXTURE PERMITTED PER SCREEN.

III. Setting - Darkwood and the Wyrd Stag Tavern

BIOME PANEL: DARKWOOD

DARKWOOD, DUSK. FOG-CHOKED OLD GROWTH. TREES ARE FLAT BLACK PLATES OVER A DITHERED WASH; FOG IS A DITHER PATTERN BAND, NOT A BLUR. LIGHT EXISTS ONLY WHERE THE LANTERN CLAIMS IT. EVERYTHING UNLIT STAYS UNREADABLE - THE PLAYER LEARNS TO FEAR THE UNLIT EDGE OF THE SCREEN.

SETTLEMENT PANEL: TAVERN SAVE-POINT

THE WYRD STAG, NIGHT. TAVERNS ARE SAVE AND RESPAWN ANCHORS: THE GREEN STAG-SIGN ANVIL MARKER IS THE ONLY PHOSPHOR-GREEN OBJECT OUTSIDE OF HEALING UI. AMBER LIGHT SPILLS FROM DOOR AND WINDOWS AS SOLID DITHERED SHAPE, NOT GLOW. SMOKE READS AS THREE STEPPED BANDS.
IV. Situations - Four Vignettes

UI/SCENE MOOD STUDIES. EACH IS A CRIT-FRAME OF ONE MOMENT, WITH THE DIALOG PANEL IT WOULD OPEN.

A. TAVERN SAVE-POINT MOMENT

KEEPER GRETA: "ROOM'S THREE CENTS A NIGHT. SLEEP AND THE ROAD'LL KEEP TILL MORNING."

> RENT A ROOM (SET RESPAWN)
> REST BY THE HOUR
> ASK FOR RUMORS

THE ONLY SCREEN IN THE GAME WHERE NOTHING CAN HURT YOU. PANEL WARMTH RISES ONE STEP; THE RESPAWN CONFIRM IS A SINGLE PHOSPHOR-GREEN LINE UNDER THE NAMEPLATE.

B. NIGHT AMBUSH

AMBUSH - PALE TIDE WOLF STRAIN: TWO WIGHTS AT THE RIDGE, FOUR EYES ON THE SCREEN EDGE. THE ONLY VIOLET PERMITTED ON A COMBAT SCREEN: ENEMY EYES AND THE VEIL WARNING TICK.

HEALTH READS AS A DITHERED AMBER METER THAT DECAYS IN 1PX STEPS. NO RED WASH OVER THE WORLD - THE WORLD STAYS BLACK; THE PANEL BORDER TURNS BLOOD RED INSTEAD.

C. SIEGE WAR COUNCIL

COUNCIL OF THE PARISH WATCH: "A STORM-CALL PINNED OVER EASTHOLD FOR NINE DAYS. THE FRONT IS CHARGED. WE MARCH BEFORE IT BREAKS OR NOT AT ALL."

TACTICAL MAPS RENDER AS DITHERED TERRAIN BLOCKS WITH GOLD/RED FIELDS OF CONTROL. THE VEIL FORECAST IS A VIOLET ASTROLABE GLYPH, NOT A METER. DEPARTURE HOUR IS CHOSEN ON A CHUNKY CLOCK DIAL.

D. CAMP MOURNING (14-DAY REMEMBRANCE)

CAMP REMEMBRANCE: "BROTHER CASS SLEPT UNDER THE ASHES. FOURTEEN DAYS HIS BUNK STAYS MADE."

THE CAMP ROSTER KEEPS THE DEAD LISTED IN DIM IRON-GRAY FOR THE FULL MOURNING PERIOD - NAME STRIKES TO A DITHERED GHOST ROW ON DAY 14, NOT BEFORE. NO MUSIC SWELL: A SINGLE STEPPED CANDLE FLICKER ANIMATION, 2 FRAMES.
V. Notes - Questions and Gaps
  • OPEN: HOW DOES THE CRT FACE SCALE TO WIDE MODERN ASPECTS - FIXED 4:3 FRAME WITH PILLARBOX BEZEL, OR FULL-BLEED? 4:3 PILLARBOX READS MORE TRUE; FULL-BLEED READS MORE PLAYABLE. NEED A CALL.
  • OPEN: DITHER DENSITY TOKENS ARE UNDECIDED - SHOULD FOREGROUND/WORLD/FOG EACH GET A NAMED PATTERN (2X2 VS 4X4 VS BAYER-8) SO DITHER IS A VOCABULARY, NOT AD-HOC?
  • RISK: SCANLINES + DITHER CAN MUD SMALL TEXT AT 1080P. MINIMUM UI TEXT SIZE AND A "LEGIBILITY LAYER" (SCANLINE EXEMPTION ON READOUT PANELS) MAY BE NEEDED. UNTESTED.
  • GAP: ANIMATION RULES FOR THE CRT FACE ARE THIN - NEED FRAME-COUNT BUDGETS (SUGGESTED: 2-4 FRAMES PER UI LOOP, STEPPED, NEVER EASED) WRITTEN AS HARD NUMBERS.
  • GAP: HOW THE PHOSPHOR-GREEN MERCY COLOR AND THE RESPAWN ANVIL MARKER RELATE - SAME GREEN OR TWO GREENS? TENTATIVE SAME GREEN, NEED CONFIRMATION.
  • QUESTION: DO FACTION PANELS GET TINTED CHROME (LIGHT = PALE GOLD BEVEL, DARK = RED-BLACK BEVEL, NEUTRAL = IRON) OR DOES AMBER CHROME STAY UNIVERSAL WITH CHARGE-GLYPHS ONLY? CURRENT SHEET ASSUMES UNIVERSAL AMBER.
  • QUESTION: WEATHER IS READ FROM THE SKY, NEVER A METER - HOW DOES THE CRT PAGE SHOW VEIL-TIDE IN UI WITHOUT BECOMING A METER? PROPOSAL: A SKY STRIP IN THE MAP SCREEN DRAWN IN THE SAME DITHER AS THE WORLD.
  • GAP: NO DECIDED TREATMENT FOR BLIGHTED-ZONE UI CORRUPTION (BORDER CORRUPTION? VIOLET TICK?) - DEFERRED, NEEDS ITS OWN VIGNETTE.
  • NOTE: PAGE A (PAINTED PULP) AND THIS PAGE MUST AGREE ON WHAT STAYS BITMAP (UI) AND WHAT STAYS BRUSHED (SPRITES). THE SEAM IS THE 2D SPRITE RENDERED INTO A DITHERED CRIT-FRAME - RECOMMEND ONE SHARED SPEC OF THAT SEAM.
