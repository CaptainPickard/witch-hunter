II.

Theme

Mood, type, palette, texture, light
Mood Statement

The Dark Court does not shout. It waits. Veil Dark is the beauty of a place that has already fallen and decided to make falling look like ceremony: obsidian halls, moon fog, red wax, an eclipsed sun hung like a wound in the sky. The player should feel watched, courted, and in danger of liking it.

Deliberate gothic, not grimdark misery. Seduction over squalor. Red is rationed like blood.

Typography Direction
  • Display: high-contrast serif with long serifs and wide tracking, courtly and cold. Uppercase for names of places and powers.
  • Body: old-style serif, generous leading, pale silver-gray on near-black. Long paragraphs allowed; the court likes documents.
  • UI numerals and labels: monospace, letter-spaced, small caps feel. Readouts should feel like inscriptions, not dashboards.
  • Rule of use: no decorative frills, no glow-heavy fantasy logotypes. Elegance comes from spacing, not ornament.

Texture and Lighting Language
  • Surfaces: polished obsidian and wet black stone; red reflections travel along floors like slow candlelight.
  • Fog: cold volumetric banks in blue-gray, always moving low; silhouettes resolve out of fog instead of walking into frame.
  • Light: hard, thin, directional. Moon shafts through tracery windows; silver rims on every black edge.
  • The corrupt sun: eclipsed, cracked, or burned-through. Appears in heraldry, scars on the land, and the blight's sky stain.
  • Red discipline: blood-red appears in under 10 percent of any frame, always at meaning points: sashes, seals, warnings, wounds.

III.

Setting

Blighted moor approach - the Veil Spire
Biome Panel - The Blighted Moors
  • Sky is a stain, not a color: the eclipsed sun hangs as a cracked disc, and ash-gray scars radiate from the fallen capital's direction.
  • Nothing clean grows. Trees are charcoal linework; the only red is burned-in sun motifs on soil and stone.
  • Weather is read, not metered: when the Pale Tide rolls in, the fog turns silver and the drain on the land lifts visibly.
Settlement Panel - The Veil Spire
  • The Spire is the old realm's fallen Solar Throne re-crowned in black: the sun-carvings were not removed, they were eclipsed with dark stone inlays.
  • Interior language: candle-thin silver shafts, red wax seals on every arch, fog pooling at ankle height.
  • Outside stands the Debt's Echo, a statue field with the faces struck flat - the court's idea of mercy.
IV.

Situations

Four scene vignettes - UI and scene mood
A - Tavern Save-Point

Rest, Rent, Respawn

The hearth is one warm coal-light in a cold room; the room key hangs as a single red seal. Save prompt reads as an inscription: "The night keeps what you leave with it." Room rented, respawn anchored, rumors overheard in the back room on faction-neutral ground.

B - Night Ambush

Wights in the Fog

Silver eyes surface in the fog before bodies do. UI stays silent; threat is shown through rim-light and eye-points. The saber's blood groove is the only red - the HUD vignette pulses once, thin, at the edges of the frame.

C - Siege War Council

Storm-Call over the Hold

A Storm-Call ritual pins a Veil-charged front over the enemy hold; the war map marks it as a dark pressure, read from the sky above the model, never a meter. Red lines on the map are vows, not paths: signing one spends the coven's favor.

D - Camp Mourning

The Fourteenth Night of Remembrance

The camp remembers its dead for 14 in-game days. A thin red ring marks the current day of remembrance; on the fourteenth night the ring closes and the marker is lowered into the fog. No UI text announces it. The camp simply stops setting a place at the fire.

V.

Notes

Open questions and gaps
  • Red discipline needs a hard numeric rule for gameplay UI (proposed: red only for save anchors, vow/seal actions, wounds, and active threats - confirm with UI design).
  • How does the corrupt sun motif scale? It currently appears in heraldry, sky stain, burned ground marks, and the Spire's eclipsed carvings - risk of repetition diluting it. Propose three tiers: distant (sky), mid (architecture), near (items).
  • Silhouette legibility against fog: sprite linework (thin silver rims) must survive at 512-1024px sprite sizes in dense fog scenes - needs a sprite test pass on the moors biome.
  • Typography: courtly serif display has no agreed web-safe fallback stack yet; current sample leans on Palatino/Georgia. Confirm system stacks for the in-game UI build.
  • Undead and orc factions share the dark court palette here - do they need a secondary accent (bone-green, grave-ash) to separate them from court nobility in combat?
  • Mourning vignette: should the 14-day remembrance have any audio cue (bell count) or is silence intentional? Currently designed as fully silent visual.
  • Blight zone art: "nothing clean grows" needs an agreed rule for what little DOES grow (ash lichen? veilmoss?) so pickups read as harvestable.
  • Weather legibility gap: Red Vigil vs Pale Tide both read as fog events at distance; consider distinct fog edge shapes (hard gold burn line vs soft silver lift).
