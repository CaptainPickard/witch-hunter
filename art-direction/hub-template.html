About This Hub

Witch Hunter is a single-player third-person open-world dark fantasy ARPG: a Daggerfall-like retro 3D world with modern action combat, and all characters, creatures, and props as 2D painted sprites with 360-degree rotation. A stylized, pixelated Elden Ring.

Everything here is generated concept art produced for the project following the locked art register (docs/planning/02 and 24): painterly pixel-art hybrid illustration, dithered gradients and film grain, massive negative space, monochromatic cool base with exactly one accent, third-person camera, held-breath mood. The sprite pipeline (doc 25) and the layered equipment system (doc 27) are proven by the pilots under Sprite Pipeline Pilots and Layer Lab.

Canonical sources: docs/planning/24-art-bible.md (frame catalog), 25-art-pipeline.md, 27-equipment-visual-system.md. Repo: github.com/CaptainPickard/witch-hunter
