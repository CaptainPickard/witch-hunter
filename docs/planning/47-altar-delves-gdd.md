## HOW TO READ THIS DOC

This doc authors the layouts, strata, perils, encounters, and boss-wardens
for the three altar delves (doc 44 Part 8, locked: the three altars are the
endgame dungeons, one per affinity; each altar = a unique long dungeon = the
chain's lever; lever pulled = witch's hand forced). It edits no ruling, no
other file, no mechanic. Layout content (strata themes, perils, encounter
placements, warden concepts) is this pass's job and is marked PROPOSED.

- NO NEW LORE: every element derives from cited doc text or is logged as an
  OPEN QUESTION with an assignment (doc 08 format). Strata layout is the
  declared exception, PROPOSED.
- IDENTITY RULES (doc 44, non-negotiable, respected throughout): the Signer
  is the WITCH's mask; the Pale Queen is the separate neutral vampire who
  leads the brokers; the light queen is Queen Maren of the Dawn (the witch).
  No beat re-litigates this.
- KNOWLEDGE LEDGER (doc 44 Part 2) governs every reveal beat: no delve beat
  lets a character learn a fact their ledger cell forbids. Section 5 states
  the standing prohibition; per-beat checks are cited inline.
- House style: no em dashes anywhere; terse GDD prose, not prose fiction.
- Encounter grammar: doc 04 (enemies use the player's verbs; courts fight in
  pairs and packs; dungeons teach a monster pool, the boss remixes it).
  Enemy pools and remix math: doc 34 (shared stat conventions, spawn rules,
  boss remix = same pool stats, size factor 3.0, two kit verbs re-costed).
- Spawn tables and sight rules: doc 34 sections 1 and 6. Light register and
  torch override: doc 02 via doc 34's torch note (20m both ways inside torch
  light). Death penalty: doc 04 locked (drop XP and currency, recoverable at
  the corpse).

## SECTION 5: DELVE SHARED GRAMMAR

Rules all three delves share. Nothing here is a new mechanic: each rule is
a citation or a PROPOSED layout application of locked grammar.

### Strata checkpoint rules (failure continues the game)

Progression safety (doc 21 progression-safety addendum, locked): every
spine chain has a failure state that continues the game; a failed court
line closes that court's unique rewards, never the game. The delves
inherit the rule: a failed strata run, a failed warden encounter, or a
failed boss-warden attempt closes nothing and ends nothing (the one baked
path exception is door 1's, stated in Section 3). Death inside a delve is
the doc 04 locked death penalty: drop XP and currency, recoverable at the
corpse (the one-corpse loop; the delve is simply a long dungeon).

CHECKPOINT BANDS (PROPOSED, GDD tuning, see AD-7): one checkpoint per
strata boundary, implemented through the doc 09/11 camp system as a
delve-side intermediate save (the warp camp system's forward-respawn rule
applies: the delve's checkpoints use the camp grammar, not new mechanics;
doc 04 save/respawn is tavern-based, camps save progress, the warp camp
becomes the default forward respawn). A strata checkpoint is a physical
place in the layout (a sealed landing, a side chapel, a cistern rim), not
a UI affordance. The delve's descent is persistent per doc 03 (procedural
interiors persist once generated; named dungeons are hand-authored and
therefore authored once). Interaction with doc 37's Cleared-Wound rule and
the R18 destruction-commits-at-exit ruling for an aborted delve run: open
question AD-3.

### Debt visibility: the one-door rule interaction

Doc 20's locked rule: debt tension stays hidden until the discovery arc;
it is never a visible meter; the player reads it only diegetically (doc
20 debt visibility; doc 19 open 1 resolution). The delves are
post-discovery content by construction (each delve's entry hook runs
through its chain's discovery arc: chain H beat 3 for light, chain D
beats 1-2 for dark, the six refusals plus the congregation for neutral).
Therefore: no delve opens a new debt disclosure. No delve beat discloses
the debt to a player who has not walked its chain's arc (the delve's
pact-scar readings and machinery are the chains' own payoff material,
readable only in the chain's context). The Veil delve's stone-terms are
diegetic reading of a debt already discovered (doc 20, doc 19 open 1), not
a second disclosure channel. The one-door rule stands: debt disclosure
happens through its own arc's door, never through a delve wall.

### Warden encounter grammar

- Source grammar: doc 04 (dungeons teach a monster pool; the boss remixes
  it) and doc 34 (remix = same pool stats, size factor 3.0, two kit verbs
  re-costed; boss taunt-resistance: bosses do not re-roll aggro).
- ENCOUNTER-GRADE wardens: one per strata, mid-boss scale, drawn from that
  strata's pool (PROPOSED placements above). They guard their strata, they
  do not gate it: per progression safety, a skipped or failed warden
  encounter does not close the descent (the next strata remains reachable;
