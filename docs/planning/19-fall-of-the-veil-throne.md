# 19 - The Fall of the Veil Throne (Continental Lore Spine)

Drafted 2026-09-12, combat/crafting/loot session pivot into lore + GDD
groundwork. Built on Nicko's core idea, expanded in collaboration.

## THE CORE MYTH (Nicko, locked 2026-09-12)

The elf-vampire court was once the SOLAR THRONE, the sole ruler of the
realm/continent. Their fall from grace came at a cost: their pursuit of
power led them to dark magic and invited evil into the world. They made
a pact with the Devil that cost them their mortality, and they were
corrupted to become vampires. This corruption is what split the
continent, creating the political power struggle that is the game's
present day.

Naming (working): the old unified realm was the SOLAR THRONE. After the
Fall, the vampire remnant became the DARK THRONE (doc 12's ruler court,
elf-vampire king and queen); the mortal provinces, Church, and Guild
broke away and crowned their own human king and queen (the LIGHT
THRONE). The blighted zones (doc 03) are the scars of the Fall: the
places where the pact burned through and the world's skin healed wrong.

## THE UNDERTOW: THE DEBT (Nicko ruling, locked 2026-09-12)

The pact was not one payment, it is a LEASE. The Undersovran realm
still collects: every generation the dark throne must pay, and the
price is escalating (memory, names, the sun, their future).

NICKO'S RULING (locked): the debt is the dark court's true motive.
They say they merely want to unite the realm to what it once was, but
the darker secret is that they NEED to in order to save themselves.
They are out of credits. Conquering the light side gives them more
souls, more land, more future to sacrifice to the creditor. The war
for the dark throne is a debtor's desperation, not ambition.

Consequences locked with this:
- The dark court's conquest rhetoric (restoration, reunification) is
  its public face; the debt is the hidden half. Dark-aligned players
  can discover it (deep-court standing questlines) and can choose to
  serve the lie or expose it.
- The Undersovran still collects from every vampire house by age tier:
  the oldest owe the most and are the most diminished. This is why
  vampire names distort the longer they are un-dead (doc 14), why elder
  vampires are barely humanoid, and why "pureblood" is the desperate
  vanity of a race in arrears.
- THE COMEBACK EVENT TIES IN (doc 13's locked evil comeback event, mass
  demon summon): when the dark court is LOSING the war, they default.
  A mass demon ritual opens the nether: demons pour into the waking
  world as an invasion. This is not a weapon they prefer to use; it is
  the bankruptcy option. Calling it down floods the world with the
  creditor's enforcement agents, buys the dark court time, and poisons
  everything it touches. A player serving the dark court may be asked
  to help light the pyre; a light or neutral player faces the invasion
  it causes.

## THE DEVIL: THE UNDERSOVRAN (Nicko ruling, locked 2026-09-12)

The Devil is NOT a waking, walking entity. He is a SPECTER OF EVIL with
a grip on the mortal world since the depths of the elf-vampire fall
from grace. He never appears as a spawn, a boss, or a body. His
presence in the world is only CONTRACTS and SIGILS: dark pacts (doc
17), the debt, the demon invasion, the blighted zones. He is a rule of
the world, not a creature in it. (Resolves the IO question from the
proposal round: historical/presence-only, reachable through bargains,
never through a door.)

## THE LONG AGENCY (approved by Nicko 2026-09-12)

The Split is not ancient history, it is LIVING MEMORY. Elves and
vampires live long enough that the first generation of survivors still
walks: living witnesses on both sides who remember when the courts
were one household.

Mechanical consequences:
- Killing a long-lived witness has REAL weight (doc 14's "elves never
  forget a slight" becomes a mechanic): kill a vampire elder who
  witnessed the Fall and every dark-court house remembers your face,
  permanently. The grudge outlives the victim.
- Some quests unlock only by bringing two ancient enemies a piece of
  their shared past (a song, a banner, a name from before the Veil).
- NPC dialogue across the world can be witness-tier: the oldest NPCs
  speak of the Fall in first person, everyone else in inherited story.
- Retinue possibility: an ancient witness can be recruited (neutral
  court contacts, or mercy-sect sponsorship), a walking piece of
  history in camp whose stories unlock lore-gated content.

## THE THIRD ALTAR (approved by Nicko 2026-09-12)

The balance the old realm kept was not political, it was CEREMONIAL:
three altars, SUN, VEIL, and DEEP, that kept the world's light/dark
equilibrium. The Fall broke the Veil altar. The true ending (doc 12's
Third Path: defeat both courts) is possible because the player, having
refused both courts, can RE-KINDLE THE DEEP ALTAR, the one both courts
forgot, held by the neutral underworld all this time without knowing
what they were guarding. The Third Path faction the player unites is
literally the congregation of the third altar.

Design consequence: the Allegiance Gambit's six refusals (doc 12) are
the ceremonial qualification: only someone who fed neither altar may
light the third one.

## THE SCHISM SECTS (LOCKED 2026-09-12, Nicko: "I love THE SCHISM SECTS; let's lock that in")

Sub-alignments INSIDE each court. This resolves doc 12's open question
about multiple alignments within one court: sect standing is the layer
BENEATH court alignment. It gates which quests appear and how a
court's NPCs treat you.

LIGHT COURT SCHISM:
- THE CHURCH MILITANT: purge doctrine. Everything touched by the Veil
  burns, including neutral villagers with one drop of taint. They will
  burn a village to save it. Militant standing comes from purges,
  witch-hunts, and unflinching zeal.
- THE CHURCH OF MERCY: redemption doctrine. Vampires can be SAVED;
  their holy scholars seek a cure for the Fall itself. Mercy standing
  comes from sparing, curing, cleansing another's taint (doc 17's
  Purify economy), and protecting the accused.
- Mechanic: sect standing is a second meter within light-court
  alignment. Quests offered, dialogue tone, and rewards differ by
  parish. The two sects compete for the same parishes; the player's
  pattern of deeds tips local parishes Militant or Mercy (world state,
  quest availability follows).

DARK COURT SCHISM:
- THE OLD BLOOD: pureblood restorationists who DESPISE the debt and
  want to unmake the pact, return to being elves, and pay nothing.
  The tragic aristocracy. They court dark players who show signs of
  resisting the Undertow.
- THE VEIL FAITHFUL: embrace the corruption; the creditor is a god
  and the debt is devotion. They welcome the default. They court
  dark players who lean into the taint (doc 17 band system).
- Mechanic: same second-meter structure. A dark player's sect choice
  shapes their endgame: the Old Blood line ends in desperate
  restoration (tying to the Lich King chain as the throne's own
  escape-the-ledger gambit), the Veil Faithful line ends in the
  bankruptcy ritual (the debt default, doc 13 comeback event).

## THE RELIQUARY WARDENS (LOCKED 2026-09-12, Nicko, placed under the Underworld questlines)

A tiny, non-conquering faction of archivists, grave-wardens, and pale
scholars preserving pre-Fall history. They are neither court; they are
the memory of what was lost.
- PLACEMENT (Nicko ruling): the Wardens fall under the UNDERWORLD
  questlines: they are part of the neutral court's web, its innermost
  scholarly circle. Neutral players finally have a faction: standing
  via artifact donation, grave-goods trade, and lore recovery, gated
  against the light-court reputation cost of graverobbing (doc 06).
- They hold: the truth of the Fall (multiple contradictory versions;
  the player assembles their own truth), pre-Split imperial ruin
  locations (dungeon keys), and they AUTHENTICATE hunter's relics
  (doc 06: a relic is not proven until a Warden signs it).
- THEIR LEADER, working name THE SIGNER: the ancient vampire who
  signed the pact as royal chancellor. She has been slowly starving
  herself by choice for every century since, trying to undo her own
  signature. See the Hidden Court below: she keeps one card.
- SUPERSEDED 2026-09-13 (Nicko ruling, doc 36 RULING PASS entry 4):
  there is no separate Signer. The 'Signer' is a role the PALE
  QUEEN has performed for 400 years inside the Wardens (she is a
  master of alteration magic; the starving is theater; she
  invented the penitent chancellor to sell the official story of
  the Fall). This section's portrait remains what the WORLD
  believes; the mask's true identity, the unmasking as the neutral
  ending's climax, and the Wardens' crisis are locked in doc 36.
  Her placement under the underworld questlines stands unchanged.
- SUPERSEDED AGAIN 2026-09-14 (Nicko identity correction, doc 36
  RULING PASS 2026-09-14): the 09-13 ruling above is itself
  superseded. THE SIGNER WAS ALWAYS THE WITCH, posing as the good
  court's queen in the present day. The PALE QUEEN is a SEPARATE
  character hiding among the neutral underground (ex-Solar Throne
  advisor, turned vampire 400 years prior, dissented and hid; she
  grants the unite-the-neutral-factions quest for the final
  battle). The doc 36/19 reconciliation session (pending) re-derives
  the Signer/Witch's masquerade, the Pale Queen's history, and the
  mask behaviors' ownership; all doc 36 opens roll into it.

## THE HIDDEN COURT (LOCKED 2026-09-12, Nicko directive; LORE REVISITED
2026-09-12, second ruling, see below)

The most faithful and most secret sect of the Third Faction: the
Underworld keeps a HIDDEN COURT OF WEREWOLVES.
- LORE REVISITED (Nicko ruling, 2026-09-12): werewolves ARE a
  corruption race/class, third alongside the Vampire and Undead (doc
  17's kit model). Like both, they NEED THE LIVING to fill their
  ranks and numbers: the bite converts. The difference is ALLEGIANCE:
  the wolf strain is NOT beholden to the good or dark sides. It is
  NEUTRAL. Lycanthropy is a corruption that belongs to no pact and no
  ledger; the Undersovran holds no claim on it, and neither court can
  command it.
- RANK-FILLING MECHANIC (parallel to doc 17's rank-filling spells:
  The Embrace, Grave Rot, Miasma): the bite converts living humanoids
  into wolves of the strain. The Hidden Court's discipline exists
  BECAUSE conversion is dangerous: an unconverted, undisciplined bite
  makes the wild wolves already roaming the world (doc 04 beasts).
  The Court converts rarely, deliberately, and by its own law (the
  trial, below); wild strains spread carelessly. Same corruption,
  two disciplines.
- THE ACE (Nicko ruling): THE SIGNER holds the Hidden Court. She hid
  them during the purges; they owe her their survival; she has kept
  them secret through every court's inquisition. The Hidden Court is
  her ACE UP HER SLEEVE, deployable in extremis: when the debt
  defaults and the demon invasion comes (doc 13), or when the Third
  Path stands at the Deep Altar, she can commit the one force neither
  court knows exists.
- BECOME A WEREWOLF (Nicko ruling): a questline where the player can
  BECOME one, run through the Reliquary Wardens into the Hidden
  Court. IO framing (proposal for review): gated to Third-Path-aligned
  players deep in the Underworld questlines (the wolf court will not
  expose itself to anyone either throne can follow home); the trial
  is the wolf's own law (hunt alone, speak to no one of the den,
  keep the human face by day); the bite is the last page. The bite
  that TAKES you in is the rank-filling conversion, given by choice
  of the Court, the disciplined version of the wild spread.
- WILD VS DISCIPLINED: wild werewolves already exist in the world as
  beasts (doc 04's beast grammar: no block/parry, leaps, high poise)
  and as the wild strain's uncontrolled conversions. The Hidden Court
  strain is what a wolf becomes with centuries of discipline: the
  player's transformation follows the disciplined strain, a
  controllable second form rather than a curse state.
- NEUTRAL BY NATURE (Nicko ruling): the Hidden Court is beholden to
  NEITHER throne. It is the corruption race OF the neutral/Third
  Faction, the way vampires are the dark court's race. Consequence:
  a wolf player keeps every neutral-court option (trade everywhere,
  the Gambit path, the Third Altar), and neither court's corruption
  mechanics (taint, holy ward interaction) treat the wolf as dark:
  Holy Wards hurt wolves only when they hunt you, not on sight.
- WOLF FORM RESOLVED (Nicko, 2026-09-12, doc 21 cross-ref): the
  wolf is a FOUR-ABILITY TRANSFORMATION KIT (Hunter's Sprint /
  Pounce / Terrifying Howl / Elder's Shape), the doc 17
  corruption-kit model applied a third time, not a 36-menu ladder.
- ORIGIN RESOLVED (Nicko, 2026-09-12, doc 22 cross-ref): the wolf
  strain is the DEEP ALTAR'S WARDENS. It predates the pact as the
  altar's guardian line, corrupted by the world's wound when the
  Veil altar broke, gone feral. The Hidden Court is the
  re-disciplined remnant, and the wolf den IS the Deep Altar's
  threshold: the Signer's ace is load-bearing for the true ending
  (the Third Path must pass the Court to reach the altar).
- NEUTRAL BY NATURE (Nicko ruling): the Hidden Court is beholden to
  NEITHER throne. It is the corruption race OF the neutral/Third
  Faction, the way vampires are the dark court's race. Consequence:
  a wolf player keeps every neutral-court option (trade everywhere,
  the Gambit path, the Third Altar), and neither court's corruption
  mechanics (taint, holy ward interaction) treat the wolf as dark:
  Holy Wards hurt wolves only when they hunt you, not on sight.
- AXIS TENSION RESOLVED (Nicko, 2026-09-12): HUNT-ANY-DURING-MOON
  RULE. Full-moon kills of ANY valid hunt target hold neutrality;
  only innocent/civilian kills drift the axis evil. The wolf is
  held neutral by the discipline of the hunt's law, not by
  target-restriction to one court. The Third Path's temptation is
  the hunger's escalation, not its targeting.
- WOLF PLAYER CLOCK RESOLVED (Nicko, 2026-09-12): INVERTED WOLF
  CLOCK. A werewolf player owns the NIGHT: night safe, day
  dangerous. The strain treats the wolf as a night creature even
  for good-leaning players. This inverts doc 03's neutral default
  and makes the Hidden Court's day-face rule (keep the human face
  by day) literal mechanics: day is when the wolf is hunted.

## Where this doc lives in the structure

This doc is the lore spine the quests/factions GDD and world GDD build
on. It does not supersede docs 03/12/13/14; it EXPLAINS them:
- Doc 03's blighted zones = Fall scars.
- Doc 12's Two Thrones war = reclamation (dark) vs. survival (light).
- Doc 12's night/day affinity clock = the pact's written claim on the
  world's clock (the treaty of night).
- Doc 13's evil comeback event (mass demon summon) = the debt default.
- Doc 14's vampire name distortion and pureblood obsession = the
  Undertow's receipts.
- Doc 17's Dark Pacts = the mortal tier of the same contract the
  throne signed; the Aegis rule = the counter-pact (the Church runs on
  the opposite currency: service, not dominion).
