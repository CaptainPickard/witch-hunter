# 12 - Moral Axis and Faction Alignment

Proposed 2026-09-11 by Nicko. Expands the moral reputation axis (11-warp-camp)
into the game's GUIDING mechanic: the good<->evil axis is what makes faction
alliances possible or impossible.

## Core Principle (locked with Nicko)
The moral axis has WORLD WEIGHT. It is not just a retinue stat: it is the key
that opens or closes entire faction trees.

## The Two Courts
The continent's factions split into two affinity families, mirroring the
axis:

DAY/NIGHT OWNERSHIP (locked 2026-09-11, affinity-relative clock):
- GOOD players: the day is theirs (towns, trade, safety in light). NIGHT is
  dangerous: undead and night creatures, evil ambushes. Sleep at night =
  risk of evil forces attacking camp.
- EVIL players: the clock INVERTS. The night is theirs (dark courts are
  awake, friendly, open). They sleep during the DAY - and DAYLIGHT sleep is
  when PALADINS and good-side forces may attack their camp. The sun is the
  evil player's danger hour; the vampire courts hunt at their side.
- NEUTRAL players: the world FORCES A CHOICE. True neutrals play the
  day/night cycle as the good side by default (day safe, night dangerous).
  Both courts will APPROACH them on the same ambush chances - the good
  side AND the bad side each demand allegiance. The ONLY way to stay truly
  neutral is to DENY swearing allegiance three times to each side (good
  three times, evil three times). After the third denial to each, BOTH
  courts start attacking them. Neutrality is not a protected stance; it is
  an achievement earned by refusing the world six times.
  See THE ALLEGIANCE GAMBIT below.
- Design consequence: the sleeping mechanic, the camping system, and the
  thief/raid threat model are all read through the player's affinity. An
  evil player's camp at noon is what a good player's camp at midnight is.

LIGHT/NOBLE COURT (accessible at good/noble affinity):
- The Free Cities of Men (and the human kingdoms/factions from 03-world)
- ELVEN factions
- DWARVEN factions
- At NOBLE affinity (deep good): full ALIGNMENT possible - access to better
  gear, standing, and protection (faction guards, safe roads, political
  cover).

DARK COURT (accessible at evil affinity past a threshold):
- ORC factions
- UNDEAD factions
- DEMONIC factions
- VAMPIRE factions
- At deep evil: these factions NO LONGER OPENLY ATTACK YOU unless provoked.
  For the first time you can SPEAK to them, complete quests for them, and
  ALIGN with them - earning the same benefits the noble human races offer
  the good path: gear, standing, protection.

## Neutral Path (locked with Nicko)
- At NEUTRAL stance: you can ENTER and TRADE with the free cities of men,
  elves, and dwarfs, but CANNOT ALIGN with them.
- Trade access without political access: the mercenary/drifters' fantasy.
- Neutral is a real playstyle, not just the absence of choice: free
  movement, free trade, no protection, no alliances.

## Design Consequences
1. The axis is a PROGRESSION GATE, not just a karma meter. Deep-good and
   deep-evil each unlock a faction web the other cannot touch. Neither side
   is strictly better; they are different endgames.
2. FACTION DIALOGUE UNLOCKS: "can I speak to this faction at all" becomes
   axis-gated. An evil player walking into an undead court is INVISIBLE to
   their aggression but visible to their diplomacy; a good player gets
   attacked on sight by the same faction.
3. PROVOKED EXCEPTION: even aligned, dark factions attack if provoked. You
   buy safe passage, not friendship. Keep this honest.
4. WITCH HUNTER FICTION TENSION: an evil player is a witch hunter who went
   over to the covens. The Church/Guild factions should treat deep-evil
   players as TARGETS (bounties, inquisitorial hunts). The good player gets
   Guild protection the evil player has traded away. Protection is one side
   of the scale; the other side gets dark patronage.
5. RETINUE SYNERGY: dark factions should offer EVIL retinue recruits
   (necromancer servants, orc guards, vampire thralls), noble factions offer
   good ones. Faction alignment and retinue collection feed each other.
6. WARP CAMP SYNERGY: camp placement near friendly-aligned territory
   (from 11-warp-camp threat model) now depends on the axis. A good player's
   camp near a human city is safe; an evil player's camp near a vampire
   court is safe. The threat model is axis-relative.

## The Allegiance Gambit (locked with Nicko, 2026-09-11)
The world wants the player to PICK A SIDE. Swearing alliance is a major
event, and neutrality must be actively defended:
- While neutral, BOTH courts approach the player (encounters, camp visits,
  envoys) and demand they swear allegiance.
- Each denial is remembered. Deny the good court 3 times and the evil court
  3 times = BOTH courts begin attacking the player.
- Until then, refusing keeps the neutral path open: free trade everywhere,
  allegiance to no one, hunted (eventually) by everyone.
- Design intent: neutrality is the hardest path, not the default. The
  neutral court (mercenaries, smugglers, underworld) is the only home left
  to a player who has refused everyone six times.
- IO note (unconfirmed): the six denials read like a loyalty meter with a
  memory. Denials could be spaced by days or by encounter; and a player who
  denied both sides 6 times but then DOES swear allegiance later might get
  special "the world remembers your defiance" dialogue and harder entry
  terms (or the courts never forgive at all - decide in GDD).

## The Third Path (locked with Nicko, 2026-09-11)
After the six refusals, a hidden QUEST PATH opens: the player can unite all
the underworld - mercenaries, smugglers, thieves, every unaligned race and
ring - and forge a THIRD FACTION capable of winning the game outright by
defeating BOTH the light and dark courts.
- This is the HARDEST path in the game and difficult to pull off.
- The reward is the ending itself: bringing ABSOLUTE BALANCE to the world,
  and this ending is regarded as the TRUE ENDING of the game.
- Design reading: the six denials are the key that unlocks the true ending.
  The world punishes refusal until the player has refused enough to be
  worthy of a third option nobody else can claim.
- NG+ ON THIS PATH: after the true ending, the player may enter NG+ KEEPING
  their camp (warp camp and everything built in it) while the WORLD RESETS
  to an even match between good and evil, with different procedural
  outcomes each run (new nobility, new map churn, new war).

## NG+ Across All Endings (locked with Nicko, 2026-09-11)
- NG+ is available after ANY ending (light court victory, dark court
  victory, and the Third Path).
- ONLY the neutral/Third Path NG+ lets the player CHOOSE their next
  playstyle: good, bad, or neutral in the new world.
- Light/dark ending NG+ locks the next run to that court's side (the
  player's standing carries as a sworn member of the winning court);
  the freedom to re-pick a path is the true ending's unique privilege.
- NG+ WORLD TILT (locked with Nicko, 2026-09-11): in an aligned-ending
  NG+ run, the ENEMY affinity court is made MUCH STRONGER while the
  player's aligned court is made SLIGHTLY WEAKER. Difficulty scaling
  rises with every NG+. From early in the run, the enemy faction starts
  capturing holds easily through sheer domination of power: the world's
  favor tips toward the enemy court naturally. This is the "tint" - not
  cosmetic, but a power imbalance that reshapes the war itself.
- Third Path NG+ WORLD TILT (locked with Nicko, 2026-09-11): the world is
  EVEN - no court tilt - BUT BOTH courts are stronger than in a first
  run, in an even way. Balance is what the true ending bought (no enemy
  gets an unfair edge), while difficulty still rises: NG+ from the true
  ending plays against two amplified courts on a fair field. Aligned
  endings get the asymmetric tilt (enemy court much stronger, aligned
  court slightly weaker).

## The Two Thrones (locked with Nicko, 2026-09-11)
The crown/nobility layer exists on BOTH sides - it is not a separate faction
but the aristocratic spine of the light and dark courts:
- LIGHT THRONE: a human KING AND QUEEN, the good side's crown. Their realm
  holds are mapped across their territories, with MINOR NOBLE FAMILIES
  (good) throughout.
- DARK THRONE: a similarly powerful RULER COURT of ELF VAMPIRE KING AND
  QUEEN on the evil side. Their holds and minor noble families (bad) are
  mapped throughout their territories.
- Mirrored structure: each throne has the same political anatomy - crown,
  holds, minor nobility - so the world reads as two symmetrical powers
  grinding against each other.
- THIS IS THE MAIN STRUGGLE OF THE GAME: playing with and aiding either
  side to completely dominate the other, changing the world as each side
  conquers the other. The player's aid drives a TERRITORY-CONQUEST meta:
  the map's political boundaries move over a playthrough.

## The Neutral Court (locked with Nicko, 2026-09-11)
CONFIRMED: a band of purely NEUTRAL factions exists as the third political
family:
- MERCENARIES, SMUGGLERS, THIEVES, THE UNDERWORLD as it were.
- A MIX OF ALL RACES living and working unaligned to any good or bad faction
  in the topworld political arena.
- The neutral court accepts ANY affinity: it is the political home for
  neutral players, and a fallback web for players drifting between courts.
- Faction mapping resolved for these groups: mercenaries, smugglers,
  thieves guild, underworld = NEUTRAL court. (Guild/Church = light; covens =
  dark; crown TBD.)

## IO Proposals (ideas from IO - unconfirmed, for review)
A. TIERS on the axis (first pass): Pariah (rejected by both courts) /
   Neutral / Honored (light) or Feared (dark) / Champion (deep) / aligned
   ("Sworn"). Crossing between tiers is earned by deeds over time; the
   visible axis makes progress legible.
B. REDMPTION IS SLOW AND COSTLY: drifting the axis back toward center is
   2-3x slower than pushing further out, and factions REMEMBER betrayal
   (attacking a faction you were aligned with is near-permanent enmity).
   Prevents easy alignment-hopping.
C. SPY/DOUBLE AGENT: a player who stays within a narrow neutral band could
   take limited quests from BOTH courts, at risk of exposure events if
   caught (a unique high-risk playstyle, fits the witch-hunter fantasy of
   playing both sides).
D. AXIS-KEYED CONTENT, not just access: each court gets unique questlines,
   gear tiers, magic (light wards vs. dark pacts), and endgame content, so
   the choice is about playstyle, not a good/bad ending binary.
E. NEUTRAL FACTIONS: RESOLVED - confirmed as the third court (see the
   Neutral Court section above).
F. VAMPIRE/UNDEG patronage has a HIDDEN COST hook (hunger, curses,
   necromantic taint as gameplay debuffs/blessings) - dark power should
   feel slightly cursed, noble gear slightly blessed. Asymmetric flavor,
   balanced power.
