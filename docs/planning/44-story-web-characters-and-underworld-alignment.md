## PART 1 - THE CAST

Each entry: identity and role, public face, true goal, what they know,

### 1. THE WITCH (Queen Maren of the Dawn / the Signer / the "Undersovran")

- IDENTITY: the peasant girl from the Fall (doc 36's truth). Four
  hundred years later she wears three masks: QUEEN MAREN OF THE
  DAWN of the light
  court (beloved, gracious, the realm's moral center), the SIGNER
  inside the Reliquary Wardens (the authority that authenticates
  relics and guards the memory of the Fall), and the UNDERSOVRAN (the
  creditor specter the dark court pays, reached only through
  contracts and sigils, never a body).
- PUBLIC FACE: the perfect queen. Genuinely protective of mortals in
  effect; rotten in purpose (doc 36's bad-in-the-good).
- HER TRUE FACE (locked by Nicko, 2026-09-14): OLD AND TWISTED, a
  real witch's face. The young and beautiful queen the light court
  loves is a MASK held by magic and manipulation; the real face
  never appears at court. Recognition cannot expose her: no living
  soul connects the radiant queen to a hag's face, and the one
  person who could have (the Pale Queen) NEVER MET the peasant
  girl. The Name in the Grave (doc 36 ruling 7) is therefore the
  only human key to her past that survives; her face is not.
- TRUE GOAL: keep the harvest running forever. The war is her farm:
  both courts' tithes, purges, cures, and grief feed her immortality
  through the siphoned altars. She does not want either court to WIN.
  A victor ends the war; a drawn war ends her hunger.
- KNOWS: everything about the Fall (she caused it), the debt (she
  invented it), the altars (she drains them), the king (she controls
  him), the Hidden Court (she holds their leash as the Signer), the
  Wardens (she leads their inner circle). She does NOT know: whether
  the Pale Queen survives in the underworld, or that the Pale Queen
  is assembling anything.
- LETS ON: maternal grace, patience, a queen above politics. As the
  Signer: penitent authority, austere memory-keeping. As the
  Undersovran: nothing at all, only contract terms.
- HIDES: all three masks from each other's audiences. Her one
  tell (PROPOSED for the reveal arcs): the grave she made for her
  peasant name (doc 36's THE NAME IN THE GRAVE).
- DOMINATION PLAN: balance by harvest. She feeds the light court's
  righteousness and the dark court's desperation in equal measure,
  collects both sides' payments (Mercy cures and Militant pyres on
  one side, the debt's tithes on the other), and quietly breaks
  anyone who approaches the truth: the Wardens fragment is her trap
  for truth-seekers, the king compulsion keeps her own throne docile,
  and the Hidden Court leash keeps the underworld's ace hers to spend.
  Her endgame vs the player: in the neutral ending she is exposed and
  the choice becomes her sentence (doc 36's locked list, re-attributed
  to the witch).

### 2. THE LIGHT KING

- IDENTITY: human king of the light throne; a good man.
- PUBLIC FACE: a strong, pious, occasionally terrible ruler.
- TRUE GOAL (his own): protect the realm and his line. (Compromised:
  his worst impulses are the witch's tiller.)
- KNOWS: the war, the church, the courts. Nothing of the compulsion,
  nothing of the debt, nothing of his wife's masks. He may sense his
  judgment failing (PROPOSED texture: he keeps lists against his own
  memory).
- LETS ON: confidence, piety.
- HIDES: (from himself) the rages and cruelties he cannot account
  for. His tragedy is that his conscience is honest and his hand is
  not his own.
- DOMINATION PLAN: none of his own; he is the board, not a player.
  His value to the story is the crack the player can find: proving
  the compulsion exists turns the light court's own crown into
  evidence against the witch.

### 3. THE DARK KING

- IDENTITY: the elf king from the Fall, cursed to vampiric
  immortality beside the queen he chose.
- PUBLIC FACE: the restorationist monarch: reunify the realm, restore
  what was.
- TRUE GOAL: survive the debt. The rhetoric is public (doc 19); the
  need is conquest-as-payment (souls, land, future to sacrifice).
- KNOWS: the debt is real and escalating; the creditor exists; the
  oldest vampires are most diminished. Does NOT know the creditor is
  the light queen (or a woman at all).
- LETS ON: aristocratic certainty, grievance worn as dignity.
- HIDES: the arrears. "Pureblood" vanity is the desperation of a
  race in arrears (doc 19).
- DOMINATION PLAN: win the war before the default. His endgame forks
  are his court's sects: the Old Blood's desperate restoration (the
  Lich King chain as the throne's escape-the-ledger gambit) and the
  Veil Faithful's bankruptcy ritual (the debt default, demon invasion).

### 4. THE DARK QUEEN

- IDENTITY: the elven queen from the Fall, the bride the king chose,

- PUBLIC FACE: co-sovereign of the dark throne, elegant, ancient,
  unreadable.

- KNOWS: the debt's schedule and costs in detail. Does NOT know who

- LETS ON: that the debt frightens her more than the war does.
- HIDES: (PROPOSED) that she has been quietly looking for a way out
  of the pact that does not run through her husband's conquest: her
  court's Old Blood sect has her sympathy, not her endorsement.
- DOMINATION PLAN: she plays the long game inside the court: keep the
  king from the default (which dooms everyone), keep the Veil
  Faithful from lighting the pyre, and keep enough of the treasury
  un-sacrificed to bargain. In the neutral ending's mid-ritual reveal
  she becomes the player's potential asset or final enemy depending

## PART 3 - THE DOMINATION WEB

How each plan collides:

- WITCH vs DARK COURT: she is their creditor and their enemy's queen.
  Their victory (conquest) pays her; their default (bankruptcy
  ritual) floods the world with her "enforcement agents" and poisons
  her farm; their restoration (Old Blood) would unmake her income.
  She manages them with escalating terms and, when they near either
  exit, escalates the war itself (her tiller on the light king sends
  the light court to the brink, which makes the dark court desperate
  enough to keep paying instead of gambling).
- WITCH vs LIGHT COURT: she runs it. The king is compelled; the
  Church's two sects both feed her (Mercy cures extract taint to her,
  Militant pyres render taint up in fire). Her only risk inside her
  own court is discovery of the compulsion: the light court's own
  piety is the weapon against her if proof exists.
- WITCH vs PALE QUEEN: the conflict she does not yet see. The witch
  holds the underworld's certification (Signer) and its ace (Hidden
  Court leash); the Pale Queen is assembling the underworld's BODY.
  If the Pale Queen ever unites the rings AND breaks the leash, the
  witch loses both her blindfold on the underworld and her spare
  army. RULED 2026-09-14 (Nicko): the witch DOES notice
  mid-questline and counters: her counter-move is to
  send the light court after underworld strongholds (purges of
  neutral dens) disguised as anti-monster campaigns, forcing the
  underworld to spend itself defending rather than assembling.
- DARK COURT vs LIGHT COURT: the visible war; conquest as payment vs
  a harvest that must not end. Neither court's victory condition is
  real while the witch stands; both "victories" are her feeding
  events. This is the doctrine's spine (doc 36): the war's two
  winners are losers.
- PALE QUEEN vs EVERYONE: she does not seek domination; she seeks
  veto power. A united underworld can lose every battle and still
  decide the war's end by choosing the final battle's ground
  (Part 4). Her vulnerability: the leash, the Signer, and her own
  ignorance of the Fall. The unite-quest is therefore also an
  investigation: to align the last factions the player must learn
  things the Pale Queen does not know.
- HIDDEN COURT: the swing faction. The witch's ace, deployable at
  debt default or Third Path crisis (locked). If the Third Path
  reaches the Deep Altar re-kindling, the Court's ace rule puts them
  on the field: whose side they take is the final battle's hinge.
  PROPOSED: their alignment route decides it (Part 4, door 1).

## PART 4 - THE UNDERWORLD ROSTER: FACTIONS TO ALIGN, AND HOW

The Third Path unite-quest (doc 12's locked true-ending structure,
granted by the Pale Queen) assembles these factions. Order is
PROPOSED; each door names what aligning it costs and what it pays.
The Deep Altar's congregation is the destination: the united faction

SEAT GEOGRAPHY (locked by Nicko, 2026-09-14): the seven factions
have PHYSICAL SEATS. Four sit in the four Great Cities (doc 03's
locked diamond): one faction seat per city, each underworld den
hiding under or beneath the ruling court's city (an underground
quarter the city's surface politics does not own). The remaining
three seats are HIDDEN LOCATIONS out in the world: abandoned
underground caverns and such, found through the doors' trust chains
and doc 41's rumor tiers, never marked.

THE FOUR CITY SEATS (assignments PROPOSED until the quest GDD pass):

3. THE UNDERGROUND DWARF CITY (light minor, deep south): the

## PART 5B - LATE-GAME PRESSURE ARCS (ruled 2026-09-14, Nicko)

## PART 8 - THE ALTAR DELVES (locked by Nicko, 2026-09-14)

The complexity correction. The altars were carrying the story's
heaviest lore weight and the least gameplay weight; this section
gives them their body. THE THREE ALTARS ARE THE ENDGAME DUNGEONS,
one per affinity: long, perilous, multi-strata descents whose final
chamber holds the altar itself. Completing the delve IS reaching the
endgame's lever; what the player does at the bottom is the chain's
true final act.

### The delves

- SUN ALTAR DELVE (light chain): beneath the New Capital. The
  Sanctified Doubt chain's climax (chain H beat 3's cold altar is
  the entry hook: the altar chamber reads wrong, the delve proves
  why). Strata descend from the city's underbelly into the siphon
  works: her machinery made physical, harvest lines, the taint
  cisterns, the altar at the bottom, ALIVE and humming. THE ACT:
  the player DESTROYS it (or severs it; final beat at quest GDD).
- VEIL ALTAR DELVE (dark chain): the ruin beneath the Veil Spire,
  ground zero of the Fall. The pact-scar's terms are legible in the
  stone; the delve reads the debt the player already discovered.
  THE ACT: destroying what remains of the Veil working at the
  bottom, the court's one act of open defiance against the
  creditor's machine.
- DEEP ALTAR DELVE (neutral chain): the longest and most perilous
  of the three, behind the Hidden Court's den (the wolf den IS its
  door, doc 19). THE RE-KINDLING IS THE FINAL CHAMBER: fight your
  way down, light the altar at the bottom. No abstract ceremony;
  the delve replaces it.

### THE LEVER RULE (locked: what the altar acts do)

The altars are the witch's power taps. Destroying (light/dark) or
re-kindling (neutral) one STRIPS HER HARVEST at its source, and
striking the heart of the machine FORCES HER HAND:

- LIGHT PATH: destroying the Sun altar cuts her tap at the light
  capital. She cannot husband the farm quietly anymore: her forced
  response is the machine's retaliation, the chaos escalation
  (Part 5B/5C), and the regicide push proceeds under her hand.
- DARK PATH: destroying the Veil ruin's remains angers her openly:
  the creditor's mask drops its patience, the tithe escalation goes
  brutal, the bankruptcy ritual's fork arrives early and hot.
- NEUTRAL PATH: when the Deep Altar is found and re-kindled, THE
  PALE QUEEN USES IT IN SOME CAPACITY TO RIVAL THE WITCH (locked):
  the underworld finally has a counter-engine, a working altar
  neither court can drain, and that rival power is what forces the
  witch's last hand: the Last Dance (doc 36 ruling 2) fires because
  the player's alliance holds a counter-engine and she can no
  longer wait. Re-kindling and rivalling are one event: the delve's
  final chamber is where the underworld's banner becomes the third
  power in the war.
