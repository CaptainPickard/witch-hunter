## PART 1 - THE CAST

Each entry: identity and role, public face, true goal, what they know,
what they let on, what they hide, and their plan to dominate or
survive the others.

### 1. THE WITCH (the light court's queen / the Signer / the "Undersovran")

- IDENTITY: the peasant girl from the Fall (doc 36's truth). Four
  hundred years later she wears three masks: QUEEN of the light
  court (beloved, gracious, the realm's moral center), the SIGNER
  inside the Reliquary Wardens (the authority that authenticates
  relics and guards the memory of the Fall), and the UNDERSOVRAN (the
  creditor specter the dark court pays, reached only through
  contracts and sigils, never a body).
- PUBLIC FACE: the perfect queen. Genuinely protective of mortals in
  effect; rotten in purpose (doc 36's bad-in-the-good).
- TRUE GOAL: keep the harvest running forever. The war is her farm:
  both courts' tithes, purges, cures, and grief feed her immortality
  through the siphoned altars. She does not want either court to WIN.
  A victor ends the war; a drawn war ends her hunger.
- KNOWS: everything about the Fall (she caused it), the debt (she
  invented it), the altars (she drains them), the king (she controls
  him), the Hidden Court (she holds their leash as the Signer), the
  Wardens (she leads their inner circle). She does NOT know: whether
  the Pale Queen survives in the underworld, or that the Pale Queen
  is assembling anything.
- LETS ON: maternal grace, patience, a queen above politics. As the
  Signer: penitent authority, austere memory-keeping. As the
  Undersovran: nothing at all, only contract terms.
- HIDES: all three masks from each other's audiences. Her one
  tell (PROPOSED for the reveal arcs): the grave she made for her
  peasant name (doc 36's THE NAME IN THE GRAVE).
- DOMINATION PLAN: balance by harvest. She feeds the light court's
  righteousness and the dark court's desperation in equal measure,
  collects both sides' payments (Mercy cures and Militant pyres on
  one side, the debt's tithes on the other), and quietly breaks
  anyone who approaches the truth: the Wardens fragment is her trap
  for truth-seekers, the king compulsion keeps her own throne docile,
  and the Hidden Court leash keeps the underworld's ace hers to spend.
  Her endgame vs the player: in the neutral ending she is exposed and
  the choice becomes her sentence (doc 36's locked list, re-attributed
  to the witch).

### 2. THE LIGHT KING

- IDENTITY: human king of the light throne; a good man.
- PUBLIC FACE: a strong, pious, occasionally terrible ruler.
- TRUE GOAL (his own): protect the realm and his line. (Compromised:
  his worst impulses are the witch's tiller.)
- KNOWS: the war, the church, the courts. Nothing of the compulsion,
  nothing of the debt, nothing of his wife's masks. He may sense his
  judgment failing (PROPOSED texture: he keeps lists against his own
  memory).
- LETS ON: confidence, piety.
- HIDES: (from himself) the rages and cruelties he cannot account
  for. His tragedy is that his conscience is honest and his hand is
  not his own.
- DOMINATION PLAN: none of his own; he is the board, not a player.
  His value to the story is the crack the player can find: proving
  the compulsion exists turns the light court's own crown into
  evidence against the witch.

### 3. THE DARK KING

- IDENTITY: the elf king from the Fall, cursed to vampiric
  immortality beside the queen he chose.
- PUBLIC FACE: the restorationist monarch: reunify the realm, restore
  what was.
- TRUE GOAL: survive the debt. The rhetoric is public (doc 19); the
  need is conquest-as-payment (souls, land, future to sacrifice).
- KNOWS: the debt is real and escalating; the creditor exists; the
  oldest vampires are most diminished. Does NOT know the creditor is
  the light queen (or a woman at all).
- LETS ON: aristocratic certainty, grievance worn as dignity.
- HIDES: the arrears. "Pureblood" vanity is the desperation of a
  race in arrears (doc 19).
- DOMINATION PLAN: win the war before the default. His endgame forks
  are his court's sects: the Old Blood's desperate restoration (the
  Lich King chain as the throne's escape-the-ledger gambit) and the
  Veil Faithful's bankruptcy ritual (the debt default, demon invasion).

### 4. THE DARK QUEEN

- IDENTITY: the elven queen from the Fall, the bride the king chose,
  cursed beside him for four centuries. The original rival (to the
  peasant girl) and the Fall's other victim.
- PUBLIC FACE: co-sovereign of the dark throne, elegant, ancient,
  unreadable.
- TRUE GOAL: survival with her court intact; possibly (PROPOSED) her
  king's soul back. She has had 400 years to study the debt and knows
  its terms better than anyone alive except its author.
- KNOWS: the debt's schedule and costs in detail. Does NOT know who
  the creditor is: the reveal happens live in the neutral ending
  (doc 36 ruling 1 stands: she learns mid-ritual, and her reaction is
  a live player-choice beat). She does not know the witch survived as
  the light queen.
- LETS ON: that the debt frightens her more than the war does.
- HIDES: (PROPOSED) that she has been quietly looking for a way out
  of the pact that does not run through her husband's conquest: her
  court's Old Blood sect has her sympathy, not her endorsement.
- DOMINATION PLAN: she plays the long game inside the court: keep the
  king from the default (which dooms everyone), keep the Veil
  Faithful from lighting the pyre, and keep enough of the treasury
  un-sacrificed to bargain. In the neutral ending's mid-ritual reveal
  she becomes the player's potential asset or final enemy depending
  on the choice beat.

### 5. THE PALE QUEEN (the neutral underground)

- IDENTITY (the corrected one): a former advisor of the Solar
  Throne, turned vampire in the Fall's corruption 400 years ago, who
  dissented from both emerging courts and vanished into the neutral
  underworld. The underworld's unifier. She grants the Third Path
  unite-quest.
- PUBLIC FACE (underworld): a patient, courtly power broker with no
  banner and no demands; the one figure every smuggler, mercenary,
  and thief respects and none can name the limits of.
- TRUE GOAL: stop the madness. She has watched two courts grind the
  continent for four centuries and concluded neither can win and both
  must be stopped by the third thing: the underworld united under one
  banner for the final battle.
- KNOWS: the continent's political reality better than any court
  strategist; the underworld's every route, ring, and grudge; that
  SOMETHING unnatural sits under the war's economics (the debt's
  shape is visible to anyone who has watched four centuries of
  tithes). Does NOT know how it all started: not the witch's guilt,
  not the creditor's identity, not that the pact's author is alive
  and crowned. Her blindness is the story's cruelest engine: she is
  organizing the one force that could end the war without knowing
  the war's author is already inside her opposition's head, her
  head's opposite number, and (as the Signer) nominally her own
  underworld's certifying authority.
- LETS ON: neutrality as philosophy ("neither throne, ever").
- HIDES: her origin (Solar Throne advisor; she knew the king, the
  queen, and the court before the Fall: she may recognize the light
  queen's face, a PROPOSED seed for her arc), the extent of her
  network, and the final battle's true scale.
- DOMINATION PLAN: not domination: assembly. Her power play is the
  unite-quest itself (Part 4). Her plan's fatal gap: she cannot unite
  the Hidden Court while the witch holds its leash, and she cannot
  expose the witch while the witch certifies her own underworld's
  relics. The player is the piece she is missing.

### 6. THE HIDDEN COURT (werewolf sect, the underworld's ace)

- IDENTITY: the secret werewolf court, corruption race #3 (doc 19).
  The Elder's Shape remembers through blood-memory.
- GOAL: survival by discipline. Convert rarely, by law, keep the wild
  strains leashed.
- KNOWS: the Signer holds their leash (they owe their survival to
  her); the doc 19 ace rule (they deploy at debt default or Third
  Path crisis). They do NOT know the Signer is the light queen.
- LETS ON: nothing; they are the best secret in the world.
- HIDES: everything, including from the player until deep trust.
- POSITION IN THE WEB: the witch's card, theoretically spendable
  against the Pale Queen's assembly, which is exactly why the Hidden
  Court's alignment (Part 4) is the unite-quest's hardest door.

### 7. THE RELIQUARY WARDENS (institution)

## PART 3 - THE DOMINATION WEB

How each plan collides:

- WITCH vs DARK COURT: she is their creditor and their enemy's queen.
  Their victory (conquest) pays her; their default (bankruptcy
  ritual) floods the world with her "enforcement agents" and poisons
  her farm; their restoration (Old Blood) would unmake her income.
  She manages them with escalating terms and, when they near either
  exit, escalates the war itself (her tiller on the light king sends
  the light court to the brink, which makes the dark court desperate
  enough to keep paying instead of gambling).
- WITCH vs LIGHT COURT: she runs it. The king is compelled; the
  Church's two sects both feed her (Mercy cures extract taint to her,
  Militant pyres render taint up in fire). Her only risk inside her
  own court is discovery of the compulsion: the light court's own
  piety is the weapon against her if proof exists.
- WITCH vs PALE QUEEN: the conflict she does not yet see. The witch
  holds the underworld's certification (Signer) and its ace (Hidden
  Court leash); the Pale Queen is assembling the underworld's BODY.
  If the Pale Queen ever unites the rings AND breaks the leash, the
  witch loses both her blindfold on the underworld and her spare
  army. PROPOSED: the witch's counter-move, once she notices, is to
  send the light court after underworld strongholds (purges of
  neutral dens) disguised as anti-monster campaigns, forcing the
  underworld to spend itself defending rather than assembling.
- DARK COURT vs LIGHT COURT: the visible war; conquest as payment vs
  a harvest that must not end. Neither court's victory condition is
  real while the witch stands; both "victories" are her feeding
  events. This is the doctrine's spine (doc 36): the war's two
  winners are losers.
- PALE QUEEN vs EVERYONE: she does not seek domination; she seeks
  veto power. A united underworld can lose every battle and still
  decide the war's end by choosing the final battle's ground
  (Part 4). Her vulnerability: the leash, the Signer, and her own
  ignorance of the Fall. The unite-quest is therefore also an
  investigation: to align the last factions the player must learn
  things the Pale Queen does not know.
- HIDDEN COURT: the swing faction. The witch's ace, deployable at
  debt default or Third Path crisis (locked). If the Third Path
  reaches the Deep Altar re-kindling, the Court's ace rule puts them
  on the field: whose side they take is the final battle's hinge.
  PROPOSED: their alignment route decides it (Part 4, door 1).

## PART 4 - THE UNDERWORLD ROSTER: FACTIONS TO ALIGN, AND HOW

The Third Path unite-quest (doc 12's locked true-ending structure,
granted by the Pale Queen) assembles these factions. Order is
PROPOSED; each door names what aligning it costs and what it pays.
The Deep Altar's congregation is the destination: the united faction
is literally the altar's congregation (doc 19).

- DOOR 1, THE HIDDEN COURT (the leash problem): they owe the Signer
  their survival and serve her leash. Aligning them requires the
  player to learn the leash's holder is the light queen, which is a
  MID-QUEST TRUTH (the unite-quest forces the first big reveal).
  Route: earn the Court's trial-law trust (doc 19), witness the
  leash's proof, choose: expose the Signer to the Court (they defect;
  the witch learns she is being hunted) or secretly re-key the leash
  (quiet, but the Court remains a double agent's card). Cost: deep
  wolf standing + one mid-quest reveal. Pays: the ace deploys for the
  Third Path at the final battle instead of against it.
- DOOR 2, THE RELIQUARY WARDENS (the Signer trap): the institution
  that holds the contradictory versions of the Fall is chaired by the
  lie. Route: standing by artifact donation and lore recovery (doc
  19), assemble versions OUTSIDE the Signer's channel, and eventually
  face the Wardens fragment: the truth-shaped trap. Walking through
  it is how the player wins (locked mechanism, re-attributed to the
  witch's authorship). Cost: graverobbing's light-court reputation
  tension (doc 06) managed all game. Pays: the archive's true
  versions: the knowledge ledger's empty cells start filling.
- DOOR 3, THE MERCENARY COMPANIES: align by contract and proof of
  pay (war effort contracts feed this, doc 42: neutral
  quartermasters buy ANY piece, coin only). Their price: the player
  becomes a reliable payer, then their banner. Cost: marks, lots of
  them. Pays: field strength at the final battle; mercenary
  requisition tiers.
- DOOR 4, THE SMUGGLERS: align by route protection and silence.
  Smugglers sell passage to anyone (doc 03); their loyalty is to the
  road. Cost: clearing their routes of the war's checkpoints (light
  and dark both tax the roads). Pays: logistics, intel, the
  final battle's approach routes.
- DOOR 5, THE THIEVES' RINGS: align by fence economy and
  non-reporting. Cost: the player's own hands stay clean of
  ring-hunts; standing with underworld fences. Pays: keys, locks,
  the descent-gate faction keys (doc 37's key system) at underworld
  rates, and den intel.
- DOOR 6, THE BANDIT WAR CAMPS: align by brokered peace between
  camps (they already sell passage to anyone, doc 03). Cost:
  mediating their feuds (deed log texture, doc 13). Pays: bodies for
  the final battle; the war's litter (doc 41 family 8) becomes
  supply.
- DOOR 7, THE NEUTRAL BROKERS: already alignable; their standing
  (doc 06) deepens stock and lowers prices through the whole quest.
  Cost: trade volume. Pays: the legendary-station clue intel (doc
  05/06), recipe trade, the Overheard Word network (doc 37).
- DESTINATION, THE DEEP ALTAR: the united faction is the altar's
  congregation. The six refusals (Allegiance Gambit, doc 12) are the
  ceremonial qualification; the unite-quest is the congregation's
  formation. The final battle: the underworld's banner against both
  courts while the player re-kindles the Deep Altar (doc 19), with
  the corrected cast positioned per Parts 1-3.

How alignment WORKS mechanically (PROPOSED, doc 12/13/20 integration):
each door tracks an underworld STANDING meter (the neutral court's
standing generalized); each door has a trust chain of 3-5 beats
(quest-GDD authored); each door's final beat is a CHOICE that costs
something real (a reveal, a betrayal, a payment, a protection);
refusing a door's cost closes that door's unique contribution at the
final battle but never the game (progression-safety, doc 21). The
doors are orderable in play except Door 1 (Hidden Court), which
requires the leash truth, which requires either Door 2's archive
progress or a dark-court debt discovery arc.
