# 11 - Warp Camp (Player Home Base)

Proposed 2026-09-11 by Nicko. The warp camp is the mid-game milestone that
turns the camp system (09-expedition-camping) into a persistent, customized
home base.

## Design Intent
Earning the warp camp is THE pinnacle moment that opens the mid-game. It
fuses the expedition loop, the cooking system, the crafting disciplines, and
faction economics into one persistent structure the player builds piece by
piece. The crafting experience should push players to build every piece of
the camp, including its decorations: the base itself is the crafting
endgame.

## The Warp Camp Class
- A camp CLASS unlocked in the early-mid game (utility magic).
- 3 TIERS of development.
- Lets the player "warp" in a camp at a location of their choosing, provided
  the site meets the OPEN-SPACE RULE (the camp placement criteria from the
  standard camping system: open, defensible, valid terrain).
- The chosen location transforms into the player's fully customized home
  base.

## Tier Structure (first pass)
1. TIER 1 - Warp Camp: warp-in a basic camp at any valid site. Bedroll/tent
   quality rest, campfire cooking, one storage chest. Saves the game.
2. TIER 2 - Warp Outpost: small structures unlock (storage hall, forge
   annex, alchemy tent, cook's tent), first NPC hires (merchant or cook),
   basic defenses (picket, watchfire). Decorations unlock.
3. TIER 3 - Warp Hold: full base building. Multiple structure types, NPC
   staff roster, real defenses (walls, traps, hired guards), decoration
   system at depth, warp-network management (see questions).

## What Lives In the Camp (building targets for crafters)
- Tents and structures of varying types (player tent, cook's tent, merchant
  stall, storage structures, workshop annexes).
- Crafting stations (forge, alchemy table, enchanting altar, workbench,
  kitchen) - a maxed warp camp duplicates the town crafting experience in
  the field.
- Merchant and cook NPCs (hired or rescued; ONE-TIME hire cost, no
  wages/food upkeep - LOCKED 2026-09-11 second sweep).
- Defenses: fences, spike lines, watchfires, traps, (Tier 3) walls and
  guard NPCs. Defense quality determines the camp's survival in night
  ambush events (see 09-expedition-camping).
- Decoration track: every craftable decoration category (banners, trophies,
  furniture, lighting, shrines) feeds a camp "comfort/prestige" value that
  buffs rest quality and NPC morale.

## Cooking Integration
- A warp camp kitchen is the top-tier cooking station (answers the station
  question in 10-cooking-meals-drinks: campfire < tavern kitchen < warp camp
  kitchen).
- FRY COOK SPECIALTY (proposed): a Cooking specialization that unlocks more
  complex dishes (4-5 ingredient recipes, higher grades, bigger bonus-effect
  pools) with more rewards. Locked-in principle from 10: recipe knowledge
  gates the ability to attempt; Cooking skill gates success odds and buff
  magnitude.

## The Retinue (player quarters)
- Tier 2+ warp camps include a special private tent/room for the player.
- Content direction: the player can keep a retinue of people they save or
  capture on their journeys, including a harem of women/men in the
  captives/retainers fiction.

## Retinue Mechanics (locked with Nicko, 2026-09-11)
Collected NPCs can serve any MIXTURE of the following roles:
1. STAFF ROLES - butler, slave, servants. They live in the camp and RUN TO
   GREET THE PLAYER when the camp is summoned (a summoning ceremony moment,
   the retinue assembles for your arrival).
2. ITEM HANDLING (Valet/Butler type) - the valet serves the SUMMONED WARP
   CAMP only, because the player keeps their PRIMARY EXTERNAL STORAGE at
   the summoned camp. The valet NEVER leaves the camp (never goes into the
   forest and comes back); they die only if killed in a camp raid, and then
   never come back. The fetch/retrieve system is INSTANT: with this NPC
   type active, interacting with them gives instant access to ALL storage
   chests built and active within the camp. The valet is a walking master
   chest interface, not a fetch quest runner.
3. ROMANCE - romanceable NPCs provide buffs (mechanics TBD).
4. QUEST SPECIALISTS - NPCs earned through questlines. Canonical example:
   the PRINCESS IN A CASTLE quest - save the princess, kill the castle
   dragon, and she joins as a servant role with a buff attached.
5. TRADE - some retinue NPCs offer trade options (vendors that travel with
   your camp).

## Retinue Affinity (locked with Nicko, 2026-09-11)
- Any NPC kept at your camp WHO LIKES YOU stays at the camp and is the one
  who actually greets you when the camp is summoned. The greeting ceremony
  scales with the relationship level you keep with each NPC.
- Affinity is shaped by how the player interacts with the world: good deeds
  and bad deeds both register.
- EVIL NPCs can be kept at the camp if you befriend them; they like you
  more as you do more EVIL things. Vice versa for good NPCs.
- AFFINITY THRESHOLD: if an NPC dislikes you past a certain threshold, they
  LEAVE your camp entirely (freely).
- SLAVE EXCEPTION: NPCs kept as official SLAVE type stay indefinitely, until
  killed. They do not leave on low affinity.
- DESIGN PRINCIPLE: the VARIETY of NPCs you collect is part of the build
  playstyle. Retinue composition is a progression axis like weapon skills or
  cooking: different collected NPCs = different camp economy, buffs, and
  quality-of-life. Collectors and min-maxers both have reasons to hunt
  specific retinue members. The player's moral playstyle (good/evil deeds)
  now ALSO determines which retinue pool is stable for them: good players
  keep good retinues, evil players keep evil ones, and mixed camps need
  active affinity management.

## Moral Reputation Axis (locked with Nicko, 2026-09-11)
- RESOLVED: a SINGLE, VISIBLE good<->evil axis, shown on the character
  inventory screen.
- The axis drives retinue affinity (good NPCs vs. evil NPCs) and feeds
  faction reactions.
- POSE/EMOTE UNLOCKS: how far the player has built toward either direction
  unlocks different character-screen poses/emotes - POWERFUL AND DOMINATING
  ones for evil, HONORABLE AND STOIC ones for good.
- SPECIAL EMOTES also unlock from certain quests or completed activities.
  Emotes/poses are a standing REWARD CATEGORY for the game's reward design
  (keep in mind for quest rewards, achievements, faction milestones).

## Summon Cooldown (locked with Nicko, 2026-09-11)
- Camps can be summoned once every 4 in-game days (LOCKED 2026-09-11,
  second sweep). Relocation follows the same cooldown: freely movable
  once 4 days have passed since the last summon - commitment preserved by
  the cooldown itself.
- This is the binding mechanic: committing to a camp location is meaningful,
  because you live with its placement for days.
- Effect: the summon cooldown binds the player to a REGION for a stretch of
  time, which is what makes defending the summoned camp in situ a real
  gameplay commitment rather than a teleport-and-leave convenience.

## Camp Threat Model (locked with Nicko, 2026-09-11)
Summoned camp danger scales with LOCATION:
- Near an enemy base or hostile faction territory: HIGHER chance of attack,
  attacked MORE OFTEN.
- In a clean safe area near friendly towns/cities: raid chance INCREDIBLY
  LOW, but a THIEF may slip into camp and steal from you.
- Thief encounters are the safe-zone risk flavor: raids are the dangerous-
  zone risk flavor. Placement is a threat-profile choice, not a difficulty
  slider.

## Thief Judgment (dynamic interaction menu)
When NPCs catch a thief, the thief is brought to the player, and the player
chooses. The option set is PROGRESSION-GATED:
1. KILL them - evil affinity.
2. SPARE and turn them in to local law enforcement - good affinity.
3. ENSLAVE them (neutral affinity) - ONLY available if the player has an
   open unused NECK CHAIN in inventory at that moment. Gate the option by
   inventory state, not by a skill tree.
4. BEFRIEND them (second chance, truly neutral) - RISK: the thief may be
   bluffing; they can eventually steal again and try to get away.
- NPC stance on slavery: some retinue NPCs are against slavery (enslaving
  upsets them), some like slavers, some are neutral. The enslavement
  affinity consequence only applies if such NPCs exist in your retinue.
- DESIGN PRINCIPLE: dynamic interaction options where availability is gated
  by progress, inventory, and world state - this pattern generalizes to the
  whole game's dialogue/interaction design (note for the quests GDD).
