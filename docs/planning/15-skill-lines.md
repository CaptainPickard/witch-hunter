# 15 - The Skill Lines (29 core + specialty expansions)

Drafted 2026-09-11, EXPANDED same day per Nicko's direction: the ~25 budget
was a floor, not a ceiling. Nicko wants MORE specialties, not fewer - the
list below is the core set; specialties (like Fry Cook) layer on top as
unlocks within or across lines. All skills level by USE (Morrowind-style).

## COMBAT (9)
1. Short Blade - daggers, shortswords. Fast, crit-flavored.
2. Long Blade - longswords, claymores. Balanced arcs.
3. Blunt - maces, hammers. Armor-crushing, stagger.
4. Axes - bleed, heavy chops.
5. Polearms - reach, spacing control.
6. Archery - bows, crossbows (dual-mode aiming: stationary reticle,
   moving lock-on).
7. Dual Wielding - off-hand attacks, combo finishers; levels when actively
   dual-wielding (levels its two contributing weapons too).
8. Shield / Defense - block stability, parry windows; levels while a
   shield is equipped and used.
9. Unarmed - fallback and roleplay option.

## MAGIC (5 schools = 5 skill lines)
10. Pyromancy - fire, heat, burning zones.
11. Cryomancy - frost, chill, freezing control.
12. Storm Magic - lightning, chains, supercharge burst.
13. Holy Wards - light magic: healing, wards, anti-undead. Axis-keyed:
    deep-good attunement only.
14. Dark Pacts - witch-magic: curses, hexes, necromancy. Axis-keyed:
    deep-evil attunement only.
Wisdom is the primary stat for all magic damage + mana (locked).
Light Wards and Dark Pacts are axis-keyed: deep-good players can only
learn/attune light magic; deep-evil only dark. (From 12-moral-axis.)

## CRAFTING (5)
15. Weaponsmithing - melee weapons.
16. Armorsmithing - armor across weight classes.
17. Alchemy - potions, poisons, oils (feeds the drink slot).
18. Enchanting - imbue gear, scribe scrolls, bind charms.
19. Cooking - meals, drinks prep, the daily buff system (10-cooking);
    Fry Cook specialty lives here (unlocks 4-5 ingredient dishes,
    bigger reward pools).

## GATHERING (6)
20. Herbalism - herbs, alchemy/cooking bases.
21. Mining - ores, gems.
22. Woodcutting - wood, rare woods.
23. Hunting/Skinning - monster parts, pelts, bones, ichors.
24. Gem Crafting - cutting raw gems into enchant components and jewelry.
25. Fishing - minor but thematic (rivers, swamp, sea). Feeds cooking.

## UTILITY / SOCIAL (3, added 2026-09-11)
26. Stealth - sneaking, infiltration, pickpocketing; powers thief-court
    gameplay, safe-camp thief intrusions, keep infiltration in the
    conquest meta, and lockpick-adjacent play.
27. Lockpicking - locks, traps, chests in procedural dungeons and noble
    keeps. (Split from Stealth so each gets depth; both feed underworld
    play.)
28. Speechcraft - better trade prices, intimidation options in dialogue,
    persuasion, rumor brokering. Charisma is its parent stat; Speechcraft
    is the trainable social skill (resolves the stat-only concern).

## SALVAGE (1, added 2026-09-14)
29. Disassembly - breaking items down into raw materials at the
    Workbench. ALL items disassemble (armor, weapons, charms, decor,
    camp goods). Levels by use: higher rank yields MORE raw materials
    per item and raises the chance of RARE-material recovery
    (higher-tier category members, catalysts, Hunter's Components at
    high ranks). Full mechanic spec: 05-crafting-system.md DISASSEMBLY.
    Also the recipe-discovery channel (05's reverse-engineering),
    relocated here as its own line's skill check.

## LUCK (stat + passive luck systems, no skill line)
Luck (stat) governs drop chances and rng rolls (locked in the stat list).
It is a STAT, not a skill: nothing to train, invest points and gear toward
it. (Nicko confirmed: luck = better drop chance.)

## Count: 29 core skill lines (28 + Disassembly, added 2026-09-14). Open-ended upward via SPECIALTIES.

## Design Notes
- Dual Wielding levels all THREE of its contributing skills (e.g. dagger +
  longsword levels Short Blade, Long Blade, AND Dual Wielding), consistent
  with the original combat doc.
- Magic schools being axis-keyed (13, 14) means a pure good build and a
  pure evil build have asymmetric spell pools - supports the court-keyed
  content idea from doc 12.
- Charisma and Luck are stats, not skills; Speechcraft is the trainable
  social line.

## SPECIALTIES (the expansion layer)
Nicko's directive: more specialties, not fewer. Specialties are unlockable
sub-paths WITHIN or ACROSS skill lines (like Fry Cook in Cooking). Known
and proposed:
- Fry Cook (Cooking): 4-5 ingredient dishes, higher grades, bigger bonus
  pools (locked in 11-warp-camp).
- Proposed specialties to review: Poisoner (Alchemy sub: specialized
  poisons/blades), Beast-Tamer (Hunting + retinue: tame companions?),
  Torturer/Interrogator (Dark court info extraction), Black-Marketeer
  (Speechcraft + neutral court: smuggler pricing), Graverobber (Stealth +
  lore: cemetery delving), Falconer/Scout (woodcraft recon). All
  unconfirmed - to be designed in the specialties pass.
