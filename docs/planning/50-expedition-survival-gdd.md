## Section 1: EXPEDITION PREP QUEST BEATS

The tavern is the expedition's airlock (doc 09: towns/taverns are the hub, the road and the deep dark are the expedition). This section authors the tavern-side loop as recurring content: five recurring tissue-layer frames (doc 20 frame grammar: patron, need, site, resolution axes, personal axes, consequence tags). Every frame is PROPOSED beat authoring; no new mechanics. All frames spawn from the World Ledger (doc 20): they are reactions to a why-now, never rolls.

Common beat loop (PROPOSED cadence): the player's expedition plan (where, how deep, how long) generates which of these frames the tavern offers. A frame the player ignores expires fails-forward (doc 20 expiry rules), never a dead end.

### Frame E1: THE PROVISIONER'S LIST
- PATRON: the tavern-keeper (doc 14 procedural roster cast).
- NEED: a supply run before a Veil-Tide front closes the road (doc 03 forecasting); the regular wagon failed to arrive.
- SITE: a roadside barn or waystop one day out on the expedition road.
- RESOLUTION AXES: (a) buy from a rival broker at a markup, (b) track and salvage the missing wagon (its beasts may have gone feral or been taken), (c) escort the driver, found alive in a den.
- PERSONAL AXES: the driver owes debts (doc 19); if salvaged drunk at a den, the player can return the goods for tavern standing or quietly keep the load (theft tag, doc 20).
- CONSEQUENCE TAGS: deed log (escort, salvage, theft), tavern standing, that tavern's supply prices for the next PROPOSED 7 in-game days.
- RECURRENCE: fires when a hold's supply meter drops (PROPOSED threshold, GDD tuning).

### Frame E2: THE MAP READING
- PATRON: the cemetery warden or a retired-hunter patron (doc 14 pools).
- NEED: read defensible camp sites before a deep push (doc 09: reading the map for camp sites is expedition prep).
- SITE: the patron's table in the tavern's back room; the payoff is in the field.
- RESOLUTION AXES: pay for the site lore, earn it with a clean deed-log page (doc 20), or steal the map leaf (evil affinity; theft evidence, and the Knock at the Door reads receipts, not intentions, doc 11 Ruling Pass 2).
- PERSONAL AXES: the patron's kin went missing near the recommended site; their token can be returned (Mercy standing) or sold.
- CONSEQUENCE TAGS: deed log, a camp-site modifier unlocked: the read site's first night-event roll one band safer (PROPOSED, Section 2 bands).
- INTENT: makes "where do I sleep tomorrow" a social, diegetic beat instead of a map tooltip.

### Frame E3: THE HIRE BENCH
- PATRON: any tavern-keeper (the bench is brokered at the bar).
- NEED: fill expedition follower slots before a push (doc 23 level-gated slots: 1/2/3/4 at levels 1/10/20/30).
- SITE: the common room.
- RESOLUTION AXES: hire at coin (doc 05/06 economy), befriend via witnessed deeds (doc 11 affinity), redeem a caught thief through the BEFRIEND judgment (doc 11), buy from slavers in dark territory (doc 23 acquisition).
- PERSONAL AXES: each candidate carries one secret (doc 14). Working example (PROPOSED): an anti-slavery bard refuses to sign on while an enslaved squire stands in the party (doc 23 slave-variant affinity consequence).
- CONSEQUENCE TAGS: retinue availability (doc 20 write-back), follower roster (doc 23), deed log hire tags.
- RECURRENCE: every tavern, always warm; the bench's candidates rotate off the doc 20 rumor economy and the doc 13 refugee flows (a flipped hold swaps the bench's cast).

### Frame E4: THE DAWN WAGE
- PATRON: a caravan master or hold courier.
- NEED: a dawn departure to beat a bandit watch on the first road stretch (doc 09: leave at dawn, not at dusk).
- SITE: the first camp site one day out.
- RESOLUTION AXES: escort through, thin the watch preemptively, or buy passage from the watch itself (neutral-court mercenary economy, doc 12).
- PERSONAL AXES: the courier carries a sealed letter and begs it stay sealed; open it or not (doc 20 personal-axes grammar; the choice writes its own tag).
- CONSEQUENCE TAGS: standing with the hold, deed log (escort/kill/bribe), the watch remembers a bribe and returns as a repeat shakedown (grudge or racket, GDD tuning).

### Frame E5: THE LEFT BAGGAGE
- PATRON: a widow-class or warden-class patron (doc 20 personal-axes cast).
- NEED: recover a prior expedition's abandoned cache: the player's own earlier camp stash, or a doomed NPC expedition's.
- SITE: the old camp site, now an encounter site.
- RESOLUTION AXES: recover the cache clean; recover and bury the dead (Mercy standing, doc 20 parish meters); recover and take a survivor in chains (doc 11 neck-chain gate; doc 23 slave variant).
- PERSONAL AXES: a survivor may be the patron's kin; their fate keys to the resolution taken (doc 20: willingness is gated by HOW).
- CONSEQUENCE TAGS: deed log, mourning hooks if a follower died there (doc 23 camp mourning system), the site marked spent or reusable.
- INTENT: the frame that teaches the loop's price: what you left behind is still in the world.

Recurrence rule for all five frames: they are tissue (doc 20); none can become load-bearing. The player who never takes them still gets the full mechanical loop (buy, hire, read map, depart); the frames are the authored texture on top.

## Section 2: CAMP EVENT TABLES

Locked ground being instantiated (no new mechanics): camp night events attract wandering threats (doc 09); danger scales with location, raids in dangerous zones, thieves in safe zones (doc 11 threat model, locked 2026-09-11); defenses actively fight in raids: guards fight, traps kill, walls slow (doc 11 OQ4 resolved); camp gear can be destroyed in night ambushes, the tent you sleep in is safe (doc 09 OQ6 resolved); territory-keyed tolerance as revised by THE KNOCK AT THE DOOR (doc 11 Ruling Pass 2, 2026-09-14, governing text).

### The night-event check
One check per camp night (PROPOSED structure, numbers PROPOSED). Modified by: site quality (defensible/hidden, doc 09), fire state (a lit fire heals faster and draws eyes), camp tier and defenses (doc 11), decoration prestige in safe zones (deterrence, doc 05 locked), the day's meal (HUNTER'S DARKBREAD's ambush-notice cue, doc 10: one warning per ambush), the day's drink or pool effects (calm-night: one guaranteed ambush-free night, doc 10 rare bonus pool), beacon-keeper's hash (ambush risk one band lower, doc 10), and the player's affinity-relative clock (doc 12 locked: a good player camps in danger at night; an evil player camps in danger at day; the tables below are written from the good-clock view and mirror for evil).

PROPOSED base bands (GDD tuning, EQ-1):
- Safe zone (near friendly towns): raid chance negligible; thief attempt PROPOSED 5 percent per night, modified down by prestige.
- Wild zone: raid PROPOSED 15 percent, thief PROPOSED 5 percent, beast prowl PROPOSED 10 percent.
- Near-enemy or hostile-territory zone: raid PROPOSED 35 percent and more frequent per consecutive night in place (doc 11: attacked MORE OFTEN); thief none (raiders do not pick pockets).
- The Knock at the Door visit: not on this table; it is commerce-triggered and reads the Ledger (below).

### Table 2.1: RAIDS (dangerous-zone risk flavor)
- TRIGGER: camp night in a dangerous band; weighted up near enemy bases and hostile territory (doc 11 locked); weighted up for wealthy, loud, well-lit camps (PROPOSED, EQ-10); weighted down by defenses and hidden sites.
- COMPOSITION: by territory (doc 03/19 spawn lists; PROPOSED working mixes): wild zone = beasts and bandits; near dark-held land = orc raiders, thrall-catchers, vampire raiders (The Embrace on downed followers, doc 04/23); near light-held land (for evil players, day raids, doc 12) = paladin parties in the pair grammar (doc 04; doc 11 Ruling Pass 2 cites the pair/pack grammar for investigators; the same grammar is assumed for raiders, PROPOSED reading, EQ-2 reconciliation).
- RAID GRAMMAR (locked doc 11 OQ4): defenses fight: hired guards engage (doc 04 player-verb grammar), traps kill, walls slow; Tier 3 camps play real base defense. Followers man defenses during your absence (doc 23). A follower or retinue member killed in a raid is dead and mourned (doc 23 locked; doc 11: the valet dies only in a camp raid, and never comes back).
- GEAR LOSS: camp gear deployed outside the tent can be destroyed in the ambush (doc 09 OQ6 resolved); the tent occupied by the sleeping player is safe.
- OUTCOMES: drive off (moral-neutral), slaughter (evil-leaning if they surrendered, deed log), capture survivors (enslavement requires an open neck chain in inventory at that moment, doc 11 thief-judgment gate pattern), losses on both sides. Whatever the raid kills or takes is written to the deed log (doc 20) and mourned per doc 23.
- AMBUSH HOUR: OPEN. Per doc 46 OQ-9, the specific hour assignment for ambushes (per court, per clock) is a pending Nicko ruling with a doc 12 reconciliation; this doc authors triggers by night-phase band only and derives no hour.

### Table 2.2: THIEVES (safe-zone risk flavor)
- TRIGGER: camp night in a safe zone; wealth and visibility raise the odds, prestige deters them (doc 05 locked: thieves pick shabbier camps; PROPOSED, EQ-10: wealth attracts in dangerous zones, deters in safe ones).
- PATTERN: a thief slips in for storage, not blood. Detection: by watchfires, guards, light radius (doc 05 lighting = safety radius), or the player waking (perception, doc 04).
- CAUGHT-THIEF JUDGMENT MENU (doc 11, progression-gated as locked):
  1. KILL them: evil affinity; the deed log records an execution.
  2. SPARE and turn them in to local law: good affinity; standing with the hold.
  3. ENSLAVE (neutral affinity): ONLY if an open unused neck chain is in inventory at that moment; gated by inventory state, not skill. Anti-slavery retinue NPCs are upset; slaver-friendly NPCs approve (doc 11 stances; doc 23 slave-variant consequences).
  4. BEFRIEND (second chance, truly neutral): the thief may be bluffing; they can steal again and try to get away. A successfully befriended thief becomes a retinue candidate (Section 4, Frame R9) and an expedition-follower acquisition route (doc 23: redeemed bandits).
- The menu is the doc 11 pattern made repeated content: availability gated by progress, inventory, and world state.

### Table 2.3: BEAST PROWLS (wild-zone seasoning)
- TRIGGER: camped in beast territory (doc 09: deep woods is safer from patrols but closer to beast territory); site selection is the risk-reward read.
- GRAMMAR: a prowling encounter, not a raid: a den's testing pack (doc 03 wolf strains), boar or bear, drawn by the fire and the food smell. Cooking without a fire kills the scent line (PROPOSED: cold meals cut prowl odds by half, GDD tuning).
- WEATHER INTERACTION (doc 09): rain kills fires and slows recovery; cold biomes demand the tent tier minimum; blizzard nights suppress prowls and raise exposure instead (PROPOSED weighting).

### Table 2.4: THE KNOCK AT THE DOOR (commerce-triggered; doc 11 Ruling Pass 2, 2026-09-14, GOVERNING over the 2026-09-14 ruling-pass territory-keyed tolerance it revises the same day)
- TRIGGER (DEED-LOG EVIDENCE): the World Ledger reads sales receipts. Selling dark-court goods at light-held markets, or light-court goods at dark-held markets, builds a paper trail; enough of it summons investigators to the camp door. The player never knows the exact threshold, only that the wrong markets remember. Thresholds, visit cooldown, ambush chance, flag decay: GDD tuning (doc 11, tracker 55).
- MIRROR SYMMETRY (locked): light land selling dark goods = paladins knock; dark land selling light goods = the dark court's enforcers knock (orc enforcers, thrall-catchers, the Undertow's collectors). Same mechanics, mirrored flavor. A neutral player selling court goods in either territory faces both courts' knocks.
- THE VISIT (HARD AMBUSH RISK): the visit IS the ambush's setup from the start: the knock, the polite questions, the inspection theater are designed to hold the player at the door while the ring forms. Unless the player proves the holding court's alignment FAST (Ledger evidence of clean deeds, deep tier of the holding court), investigators draw steel mid-conversation (light investigators use the pair grammar, dark enforcers the pack grammar, doc 34 spawn rules as cited by doc 11).
- THE OUT: refusing the door or spotting the ring early (perception, Warden intel, watching the treeline) avoids the ambush but leaves a SUSPICION FLAG on the camp's file: future visits come armed, and the flag decays only with clean time and clean markets.
- PROOF = REPUTATION: the interrogation tests reputation, not housekeeping. A deep-good player in light land talks their way through (their witnessed deed record is the proof); an evil player's file convicts them whatever they carry. Hiding goods does not help: the receipts already exist.
- AMBUSH HOUR: OPEN per doc 46 OQ-9 (same flag as Table 2.1): the visit's hour assignment is not derived here.
- INTERFACE NOTES (PROPOSED, for UI art when authored): the knock presents as a dialogue scene with an inspection window; the player's exits are answer, refuse, or leave through the back (a perception-gated read of the ring, doc 04 perception grammar).

Design principle for all tables (locked doc 11): placement is a threat-profile choice, not a difficulty slider. The tables above give each placement its profile; nothing scales to the player.

## Section 3: WARP CAMP PROGRESSION CONTENT

Doc 11's three tiers as authored content. All numbers PROPOSED (GDD tuning, EQ-5). Locked rules carried unchanged: 3 tiers, open-space placement rule, one warp camp per character in v1, 4-day summon/relocation cooldown, one-time hire cost with no upkeep, defenses actively fight, no fast travel through camps, the unlocked warp camp is the default forward respawn.

### Tier 1: WARP CAMP
- UNLOCK BEAT: the utility-magic class unlock in the early-mid game (doc 11); earned, not bought (the warp spell itself, PROPOSED acquisition: a warp-camp charter from a Guild requisition line or a dungeon find, PROPOSED beat, EQ-5).
- STRUCTURES: bedroll/tent-quality rest, campfire, one storage chest, the Field Kit crafting tier (doc 05 station tier 1: ceiling STANDARD, T1-T2 expedition work). Saves the game.
- COOKING: campfire tier only: 3-ingredient meals (doc 10 locked station ladder).
- CRAFTING GATES: structures and the Field Kit built from gathered materials through the camp system itself (doc 05: camping gear and warp camp structures are not a skill line's product); supplies consumed per the Workbench chain where the player has one, raw materials otherwise at this tier (PROPOSED simplification at Tier 1, EQ-5).

### Tier 2: WARP OUTPOST
- UNLOCK BEATS (crafting gates per doc 05's station ladder): each structure is a Workbench project consuming supplies (leather grades, lumber, fittings, cloth per doc 05 supply chains); PROPOSED gate: Workbench at Village tier (ceiling FINE) to raise structures.
- STRUCTURES UNLOCKED: storage hall (all-chest access consolidates here), forge annex (Village smithy, refine T3), alchemy tent (Village alchemy table: standard potions, coatings T1-T3), cook's tent (tavern-kitchen equivalence: 4th-ingredient grading unlocked in the field, doc 10 station ladder), player's private quarters (the retinue tent, doc 11 Tier 2+), picket lines and watchfires (basic defenses), first decoration track opens.
- NPC HIRES: the merchant or the cook (doc 11: hired or rescued; one-time cost, no upkeep, locked). First staff roster begins; the retinue tent makes romance/retinue fiction available (doc 11 retinue roles).
- PARTY MENU: the follower menu installs here (doc 23 locked: view stats, equip gear; usable at camps and taverns).

### Tier 3: WARP HOLD
- UNLOCK BEATS: full base building. Grandmaster station projects (doc 05: the Grandmaster forge and Grandmaster lab are buildable ONLY at a maxed warp camp): the camp's crafting endgame, exceeding every world station except the single legendary dungeon station.
- STRUCTURES: full structure catalog (doc 05 decoration category 8), walls and trap lines and guard posts (real base-defense gameplay at Tier 3, doc 11), the Workbench as the sixth station at Grandmaster tier (feeds every line; line-stations stop refining), kitchen station at the top of the cooking ladder (doc 10).
- NPC STAFF ROSTER: staff, valet, merchant, cook, hired guards (doc 11, doc 23; the retinue resident layer, uncapped except by camp capacity, doc 11 Ruling Pass 2026-09-14).
- DECORATION SYSTEM AT DEPTH: the full eight categories below.
- WARP-NETWORK MANAGEMENT: doc 11 Tier 3 names it; multiple camps are not in v1 (doc 11 OQ1 resolved). Carried open (EQ-7): the Tier 3 warp-network management beat stays parked until multi-camp design exists.

### The Workbench and supplies (locked doc 05; instantiated here)
- THE SIXTH STATION: the Workbench refines/purifies world ingredients into USABLE SUPPLIES (pelts > leather grades, woods > lumber, ores > ingots, fiber > cloth, fittings, pigments + biome dyes, purified reagents at 3:1). Gear, food, potions, decorations, structures consume supplies, not raw materials.
- TIERS: Field Kit (T1-T2 refine) to Grandmaster (refine everything), the doc 05 five-tier ladder; station tier x skill tier = supply grade, feeding every downstream craft's grade roll.
- ALL-CRAFTABLE PRINCIPLE (locked): everything craftable at the camp except two locked exceptions: Hunter's Relics (doc 06, Reforge-only) and spine-story items (doc 20 spine immunity). Disassembly is core skill line 29 (doc 05, locked 2026-09-14): all items break down at the Workbench into raw materials.

### The eight decoration categories (locked 2026-09-12, doc 05/11) and prestige
Categories, all Workbench-crafted:
1. RUGS/FLOORS (hides, woven fiber, per-biome pattern sets from doc 03 pigments).
2. TROPHIES (monster-part-first, doc 06): every biome enemy has a craftable trophy; TROPHY BUFFS locked: an apex-beast trophy grants a small permanent camp-wide buff vs that beast family while displayed.
3. FURNITURE (beds, tables, benches, Tier 3 thrones).
4. LIGHTING (sconces, witchfire lanterns, consecrated lamps; light = night safety radius, doc 12 clock).
5. BANNERS + HERALDRY (charged-shields grammar, doc 03; OPTIONAL display per player choice, locked).
6. SHRINES (axis-gated: light shrine, dark altar, neutral hearth; feed rest quality per affinity, doc 12).
7. DEFENSES (locked doc 11, now Workbench-crafted: spikes, traps, walls, watchfires).
8. STRUCTURES (tents to halls, all doc 11 tiers).

DECORATION PRESTIGE (locked, doc 05/11): every category feeds camp comfort/prestige, which buffs:
1. REST QUALITY: full heal; the morning buff slot extends one extra hour per prestige band.
2. GREETING CEREMONY scale (doc 11's retinue-assembles fantasy, made mechanical).
3. SAFE-ZONE THIEF DETERRENCE (thieves pick shabbier camps, doc 05 locked; wealth attracts raids in dangerous zones, PROPOSED, EQ-10).
4. CAMP AS STATION: a Masterwork-decorated Tier 3 camp adds +1 grade roll to meals and potions brewed there.
5. RETINUE AFFINITY TOLERANCE: prestige loosens the leave-on-dislike threshold one band (doc 11 affinity rules; doc 23 mourning/blame interplay rides the same tolerance).

Progression shape (PROPOSED beat order): Tier 1 makes the expedition sustainable; Tier 2 makes it comfortable and begins the retinue; Tier 3 makes the camp a town that happens to be yours. The crafting experience pushes players to build every piece including decorations: the base is the crafting endgame (doc 11 design intent).

## Section 4: RETINUE CONTENT BEATS

Doc 23's four follower types plus doc 11's retinue roles as authored recruitment beats. Ten authored frames total (R1-R10, the tenth being the variant frame), PROPOSED, in doc 20 grammar. Each frame lists: where the NPC is found, affinity lean (good/evil/neutral, doc 11/12 single visible axis), camp role (staff/valet/romance/quest-specialist/trade), and the deed-log tags the recruitment writes. Locked rules carried: the valet is camp-bound and never fetches (doc 11 OQ9 resolved); one-time hire cost, no upkeep (doc 11 locked 2026-09-11); follower meal upkeep: none (doc 23 locked); followers live at the camp, the expedition party is who walks out, up to 4 level-gated (doc 23).

### Frame R1: THE ROADSIGNING BARD
- FOUND: taverns across both realms; also shadow-court dens (doc 23 bard acquisition: befriended and recruited from taverns and world locations).
- AFFINITY LEAN: any; their affinity IS their kit (doc 23 locked: good bards sing protection, evil bards buff damage and coat weapons, neutral bards carry the travel kit).
- CAMP ROLE: staff (performance at the greeting ceremony); expedition bard when designated.
- DEED-LOG TAGS: befriended-bard, patron-court tag from where they were recruited.
- ROMANCE-CAPABLE (doc 11); buff skeleton PROPOSED in Section 6 (EQ-4).

### Frame R2: THE TAKEN SQUIRE
- FOUND: a slaver caravan in dark territory (doc 23: bought from slavers), or freed in a raid on one.
- AFFINITY LEAN: reads the player's axis, not its own: an enslaved squire's affinity is a management problem (doc 23 slave variant: obeys identically, never leaves on low affinity, upsets anti-slavery retinue NPCs).
- CAMP ROLE: expedition squire (inventory manager; carries the overflow pool, tends camp chores, holds the torch).
- DEED-LOG TAGS: enslavement (dark deed), rescue variant (good deed if freed rather than taken). The Knock at the Door reads the receipts of a slaver-sold retinue like any commerce (doc 11 Ruling Pass 2; PROPOSED reading, EQ-2 reconciliation if courts weigh retinue provenance).

### Frame R3: THE DISGRACED GUARD
- FOUND: a light-held hold's road tavern; a deserter or cashiered garrison soldier drinking on a bad name.
- AFFINITY LEAN: good or neutral; leans by why they left (an honorable refusal reads good, a rout reads neutral).
- CAMP ROLE: expedition guard candidate (front line, taunt-tier attention pulling, shield-wall stance per doc 23); camp guard at Tier 3 when not deployed.
- DEED-LOG TAGS: hired-guard (neutral), redemption arc if their desertion is cleared by a frame's exposure resolution (deed log: honor restored, standing +).
- GEAR HOOK: the player equips them (Workbench-crafted, doc 23 gear-gated); a real gold-and-attention sink.

### Frame R4: THE HEDGE ARCANIST
- FOUND: rescued from a Militant-flip purge in a parish that flipped past -50 (doc 20 parish rules), or bought out of a neutral-court den (doc 03 shadow court).
- AFFINITY LEAN: any, keyed to their school (doc 17: schools strictly axis-gated; Holy/Dark gated to the holder's axis).
- CAMP ROLE: expedition arcanist (back line, zone control, one heal or hex per axis); camp scroll-scribe at Tier 2+ (Enchanting-adjacent service, PROPOSED camp service menu, EQ-6).
- DEED-LOG TAGS: rescue (good), purchase (neutral), harboring (dark, if pulled from a den).

### Frame R5: THE CAMP VALET
- FOUND: neutral-court brokers in either market (PROPOSED one-time cost band, EQ-6).
- AFFINITY LEAN: neutral by trade; affinity managed like any retinue NPC (doc 11), with the slave exception unavailable (a valet is a hire, not a captive; PROPOSED reading).
- CAMP ROLE: valet/butler (doc 11 ITEM HANDLING): serves the SUMMONED warp camp only; NEVER leaves camp, never fetches; interacting gives instant access to ALL storage chests built and active in the camp; a walking master-chest interface, not a fetch runner. Dies only in a camp raid, never returns.
- DEED-LOG TAGS: hire (neutral commerce). No upkeep (locked).

### Frame R6: THE ROADSIDE MERCHANT
- FOUND: a merchant whose caravan the player escorted or salvaged (Frame E1 personal-axes payoff), or hired at city markets.
- AFFINITY LEAN: neutral; their stock leans by home market (light-held markets stock light-court goods and vice versa: the commerce the Knock reads, doc 11 Ruling Pass 2).
- CAMP ROLE: trade (a vendor that travels with the camp, doc 11 role 5); never deploys.
- DEED-LOG TAGS: hire; their stall's receipts enter the player's Ledger trail (selling court goods at the wrong market builds the paper trail).

### Frame R7: THE CAMP COOK
- FOUND: a tavern cook bought out of a post (paid service inverted: doc 10's tavern cooks cook for you; the best of them can be hired out of it), or a FRY COOK specialty NPC (doc 16).
- AFFINITY LEAN: good or neutral.
- CAMP ROLE: staff (runs the warp-camp kitchen station when the player is away; cooks FOR the party, PROPOSED service menu, EQ-6); a FRY COOK hire unlocks the specialty's 4-5-ingredient dishes as a camp service (doc 10/11).
- DEED-LOG TAGS: hire.

### Frame R8: THE PRINCESS IN A CASTLE
- FOUND: the canonical doc 11/20 quest: save the princess, kill the castle dragon.
- AFFINITY LEAN: good.
- CAMP ROLE: quest-specialist joining as a servant role with a buff attached (doc 11 role 4); romance-capable.
- PERSONAL AXES (doc 20, locked grammar): save her by honorable combat and she joins WILLINGLY (romance/retinue fiction); slay the dragon while it still holds her in terror and she can still be ENSLAVED (neck-chain requirement, NPC slavery stances applied); kill the dragon, free her, escort home: no retinue gain, but the house remembers the honor (standing + deed log).
- DEED-LOG TAGS: rescue, enslavement, or honorable-escort, per resolution taken.

### Frame R9: THE REDEEMED THIEF
- FOUND: the thief judgment menu (Section 2, Table 2.2), BEFRIEND route.
- AFFINITY LEAN: neutral; truly neutral, second chance.
- CAMP ROLE: staff or expedition squire variant; their fingers are their kit (PROPOSED camp service: lockwork and discreet retrieval, EQ-6).
- RISK (locked doc 11): they may be bluffing; they can eventually steal again and try to get away. A betrayal is a deed-log event the camp's mourners and gossip remember (doc 23 living-camp principle).
- DEED-LOG TAGS: mercy (neutral/good lean), repeat-theft grudge if they relapse.

### Frame R10: THE COURT-GIVEN FOLLOWER
- FOUND: granted by a court at a standing threshold (doc 23 acquisition: granted by courts); a light-aligned squire from the Militant, or an orc guard from a dark court at deep alignment (doc 12: dark factions offer evil retinue recruits, noble factions good ones).
- AFFINITY LEAN: the granting court's.
- CAMP ROLE: expedition follower of the archetype the court grants (guard, squire, bard, arcanist per doc 23).
- DEED-LOG TAGS: court-grant (standing-gated; losing the standing can dismiss them, PROPOSED rule, EQ-6).

Recruitment write-back rule (locked doc 20): every frame's ending writes consequence tags to the World Ledger (deed log, standing, retinue availability), which changes what spawns next; a befriended thief's relapse, a freed princess's house's gratitude, a bought squire's anti-slavery friction are all Ledger entries first and dialogue second.
