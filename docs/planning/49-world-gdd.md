## SECTION 1: THE UNDERWORLD CAPITAL (PLACEMENT)

Locked shape (doc 44 Part 4): THE PALE QUEEN'S SEAT, the UNDERWORLD
CAPITAL. A massive cavern-city deep in the continent, tiers of
dwellings carved into rock walls, the Deep Altar's congregation
forming around her court. It is the underworld's counter to the four
surface capitals and where the final battle's banner musters.

### 1.1 Physical anchor (PROPOSED)

The capital sits under the CONTINENT'S DEEP INTERIOR: beneath the
high central spine of old imperial highlands that separates the
New Capital's south-center light territory from the Veil Spire's
north-center dark territory (doc 03 continent; doc 22 locked
diamond). Reasoning: the Pale Queen is the underworld's counter to
BOTH throne territories, not one realm's basement. Placing her
under the neutral spine between the two courts keeps her equidistant
from Maren's seat and the Veil Spire, matching her neutral vampire
standing (doc 36: separate from both courts, leading the brokers'
web) and matching the underground dwarf city's deep-south dig
grammar (doc 22: deep works, dwarf hands).

Depth: three days' delve beneath the spine's roots, past the last
worked imperial tunnel. The cavern is a drowned-then-drained sea
cave system, which explains the tiered rock walls: each tier is an
old waterline (PROPOSED; no doc fixes the geology).

### 1.2 Surface access points

NONE MARKED. Every mouth is rumor-only (doc 37/41: hidden seats
found through doors' trust chains and rumor tiers, never marked).
The known surface touches (PROPOSED, each rumor-tier gated):

- THE DROWNED STAIR: an old imperial well-shaft behind a ruined
  waystation on the spine's south face. Rumor tier 3 gate: only a
  broker-paid rumor names it; the waystation reads as ordinary
  ruin on the map (doc 41 grammar).
- THE TITHER'S HOLE: a mineshaft lift east of the Underground
  Dwarf City's deep-south workings, half-abandoned, run by a
  thieves' ring splinter that pays the queen's court toll passage.
  Rumor tier 2 gate (doc 44 Part 4: the dwarf city holds the
  thieves' rings' seat; the rings know the lift).
- THE WOLF-ROAD MOUTH: the eastern massif's lower caves, reached
  only behind the Hidden Court's trust (Section 2). Not a public
  access: it is the congregation's own pilgrimage door.

### 1.3 Relationship to the three hidden seats

Doc 44 Part 4 locks the three hidden seats. The capital's
placement relative to each (PROPOSED distances, locked seats):

- HIDDEN COURT DEN (eastern massif, at the Deep Altar's
  threshold): the capital's WOLF-ROAD runs east beneath the
  old imperial tunnels to the massif's roots. The congregation's
  pilgrimage path. The Court answers to the queen's discipline
  (doc 19: the re-disciplined remnant), so the road is guarded
  at both ends and open only to trusted walkers.
- RELIQUARY WARDENS' VAULT-SEAT (a cavern archive deep
  underground): placed (PROPOSED) under the western reaches of
  the spine, west of the capital, one day's delve along the
  drowned-then-drained sea-cave system's western branch. The
  Wardens archive what the courts would burn; distance is their
  defense, and the capital's neutrality is their guarantee.
- NEUTRAL BROKERS' TRUE SEAT (an abandoned cavern market at a
  crossroads of old imperial tunnels, reachable only by rumor):
  placed (PROPOSED) at the spine's north-central tunnel
  crossroads, between the capital and the Veil Spire's territory,
  two days' delve from the capital by the old imperial trunk
  line. The Pale Queen leads the brokers (doc 36); the seat is
  deliberately NOT under her capital: her broker hand and her
  crown are kept apart so no broker customer ever meets the
  queen's court.

### 1.4 Travel grammar (how the player first reaches it)

Per doc 37/41: never marked, rumor tiers only. First-reach
grammar (PROPOSED, per locked seat rules):

1. The player wins a DOOR's trust (doc 44 Part 4: hidden seats
   are found through doors' trust chains). The working chain
   (PROPOSED): a smuggler contact at Veil Spire names a
   thieves' ring fixer in the Underground Dwarf City; the fixer
   sells the Tither's Hole lift token at rumor tier 2; three
   toll-paid rides down and the player stands in the tier
   walls, unmarked, uninvited, watched.
2. Arrival is into the LOWEST tier (the market tier), never the
   court tier. The queen's court is met only after the Deep
   Altar congregation vouches (Section 2 grammar), or after the
   player carries a Warden's sealed slate from the vault-seat.
3. No map marker is ever granted. The hold overlay (doc 13)
   shows nothing here: the capital is outside conquest
   territory by law (doc 44 Part 4: the underworld's counter
   seat, not a conquest node).

### 1.5 Mustering role (locked, placement note)

The final battle's banner musters here (doc 44 Part 4). The
muster ground (PROPOSED): the DROWNED FLOOR, the capital's
lowest drained tier, wide enough to hold every faction's
banner. The congregation's pilgrimage road (Section 2) is the
muster's artery: banners travel the wolf-road to the Deep
Altar for the re-kindling march.

## SECTION 2: THE DEEP ALTAR RELATIVE TO THE CAPITAL

Doc 44 Part 4 leaves "how the Deep Altar sits relative to it" PROPOSED
at this pass. Doc 19 locks the den: the Hidden Court's den is beneath
the eastern massif AT THE DEEP ALTAR'S THRESHOLD; the wolf den IS the
altar's door. Doc 08 (line 104-111): the Deep Altar's dedication is
LOCKED, built down-deep to the world's own deeps, never a throne
instrument.

### 2.1 Placement (PROPOSED)

The Deep Altar lies beneath the EASTERN MASSIF's deepest roots, below
and beyond the Hidden Court's den. The den is the threshold hall: to
enter the altar's presence the walker passes through the Court's
floor, past the wardens' dais, and down the STAIR OF TONGUES (PROPOSED
name; a stair of old altar-mason cut, dwarf-work; PROPOSED/OPEN
QUESTION WQ-6 that the dwarf-work predates the pact: doc 08 line
104 confirms dwarven altar-masons but not the stair's age), a
further half-day's descent.

Depth relationship to the underworld capital (PROPOSED): the altar is
DEEPER than the capital, not adjacent to it. The capital hangs in its
own great cavern off the spine; the altar reaches the world's own
deeps (locked dedication grammar) under the eastern massif, five
days' delve from the capital by the wolf-road, and the last day is
the Stair itself. The altar is thus the deepest fixed point in the
world's geography: below the surface capitals' delves, below the
underworld capital's lowest tier, below the vault-seat's archive
depth. Every road ends near it; only one road ends at it.

### 2.2 Pilgrimage grammar (congregation travels from capital to altar)

Doc 44 Part 4: the Deep Altar's congregation forms around the Pale
Queen's court. The congregation therefore LIVES in the capital and
WALKS to the altar (PROPOSED cadence):

- THE WOLF-ROAD: the guarded underground artery from the capital's
  court tier east to the massif's roots. One day per season the
  road opens fully: the CONGREGATION'S WALK, the whole court
  traveling to the altar for the season's rite. Walkers go in
  silence past the tier where the wild strain dens pool at the
  ruins' edges (doc 08 line 110: werewolf wild-strain dens pool at
  all three altar ruins' edges, broken wards).
- The Court itself does not leave the threshold: the wardens' line
  predates the pact as the altar's guardian strain (doc 19, locked);
  they hold the den while the congregation walks past them down.
- A player in the congregation's trust may walk the road in a
  Walk's wake: the road is open, the Court tolerates a vouched
  walker, and the altar's door (the den) is the test.

### 2.3 The siege approach (door 1's leash-truth-known failure fires)

Doc 44's door-1 grammar: the leash-truth-known failure fires a siege
version of the re-kindling. Siege approach grammar (PROPOSED, within
the locked re-kindling frame):

- The siege does not dig a new door. The attacking host must come
  the way the congregation walks: the WOLF-ROAD is the only artery,
  so the siege is a ROAD FIGHT, not a wall fight. The Court's
  wardens fight the road's defense in the den and on the Stair;
  the congregation's fight is the re-kindling march itself.
- The capital's role in the siege: the banner musters on the
  Drowned Floor (Section 1.5) and marches the wolf-road as a
  column. If the road is cut at the middle tiers, the muster
  shatters into faction camps along the old imperial tunnels.
- Failure state: if the altar is reached by the hostile host, the
  re-kindling fails into the siege's second grammar: the altar
  unlit, the congregation holding the Stair and the two tier
  (PROPOSED chokepoints: the Drowned Stair and the Tither's Hole
  are the two mouths that can be sealed from below).

## SECTION 3: THE THREE ALTAR SITES (SURFACE AND DEPTH PLACEMENT)

Doc 19 (locked): SUN, VEIL, DEEP kept the world's equilibrium; the
Fall broke the Veil altar. Doc 08 line 107-110: Deep Altar dedication
locked; wild-strain dens pool at all three ruins' edges. Placement
pass on each (sites locked; anchors, covers, routes PROPOSED):

### 3.1 SUN ALTAR: beneath the New Capital

Locked: beneath the New Capital, her tap (doc 36: it still stands
because she keeps it standing; it is drained, not desecrated); delve
entry from the city's underbelly (doc 44 Part 8).

- Physical anchor (PROPOSED): directly beneath the New Capital's
  palace hill, in the old altar-mason cut that predates the city.
  The city grew on top of it deliberately: the light court built
  its capital over its own altar's tap.
- Delve mouth (locked anchor, PROPOSED cover): entry from the
  city's underbelly, a sewer-shrine district door the surface
  world believes is a closed plague crypt. The city's poor leave
  offerings at the door without knowing what stands below.
- Route from the underworld capital (PROPOSED): THE SMUGGLER ROAD,
  the old imperial trunk line south from the spine to the New
  Capital's deep cellars. Doc 44 Part 4: the Veil Spire holds the
  smugglers' seat, so the road's toll is paid in smuggler credit:
  the DOOR THAT PAYS THIS ROUTE is the smugglers' seat's door.
  The route is the longest surface-capital road (four days' delve)
  and the most policed by light patrols above.
- Note (locked, doc 36): the Sun altar is drained, not desecrated:
  the site reads as exhausted, not ruined. No blight scar here
  (doc 08: three scars are at the Spire, Moor March, Darkwood
  Eaves).

### 3.2 VEIL ALTAR RUIN: beneath the Veil Spire

Locked: beneath the Veil Spire, ground zero of the Fall.

- Physical anchor (PROPOSED): beneath the Veil Spire's root, at the
  north-center of the continent (doc 22: dark capital,
  north-center). The Spire's foundations are built INTO the ruin:
  the dark court's capital is a city roofing its own wound.
- The CORE SCAR (doc 08 lines 83-89: resolved, three scars, Core
  ~10 km2 at the Spire): the Fall's surface expression. The altar
  ruin is the scar's root. Delve mouths into the ruin open at the
  scar's edges where the wards broke (doc 08 line 110: wild-strain
  dens pool at the ruin's edges).
- Delve mouth cover (PROPOSED): the surface reads the scar as a
  blighted war-ground with a sealed Spire core; the actual delve
  mouth the player uses is a collapsed Spire under-croft that
  smugglers already use for storage. The dark court pretends the
  mouth does not exist; it is their shame door.
- Route from the underworld capital (PROPOSED): THE NORTH TRUNK,
  the old imperial line north through the Neutral Brokers'
  crossroads seat (the brokers' seat sits ON this route, Section
  1.3), then down the Spire's old service workings. The DOOR THAT
  PAYS THIS ROUTE: the brokers' door, whose toll is information,
  not coin. The Warden pilgrim path also touches this route's
  western branch (the vault-seat's archive feeds the brokers'
  market).

### 3.3 DEEP ALTAR: behind the Hidden Court's den, under the eastern massif

Locked: doc 19 (den IS the altar's door); doc 08 line 106-108
(massif = old quarries; dedication locked to the world's own deeps).

- Physical anchor (PROPOSED): the eastern massif's deepest roots,
  under the old quarry galleries. The massif reads on the surface
  as scarred highland: quarried terraces at its flanks (doc 08
  map grounding), wolf country above the treeline, the Court's
  den a day's delve in from the quarry mouths.
- Delve mouth's diegetic cover (PROPOSED): the surface world
  believes the quarry terraces are exhausted imperial quarries
  with wolf trouble. Hunters' rumor tier 1 says wolves; tier 2
  says the wolves are wrong somehow; tier 3, held only by doors'
  trusted, says the wolves are a court and the mountain is a
  door. Nobody's map marks anything.
- Route from the underworld capital (PROPOSED): THE WOLF-ROAD,
  Section 2's guarded artery, two days' common delve then the
  threshold day. The DOOR THAT PAYS THIS ROUTE: the Hidden
  Court's door; passage is paid in trust, not toll: the Court
  must know the walker's name (doc 19 grammar).

### 3.4 Inter-site triangle (PROPOSED distances)

- New Capital (Sun) to Veil Spire (Veil): the surface route around
  the scars is six days; the delve route under the spine is four
  days (the capital sits on it, Section 1).
- Veil Spire to eastern massif (Deep): three days' delve by the
  old imperial east branches, past wild-strain den edges.
- New Capital to eastern massif (Deep): the combined descent runs
  nine days when both roads are walked (four via the smuggler road
  alone, five via the wolf-road alone); the shortest surface-to-Deep
  walk for a light-court player runs through the smuggler road and
  the wolf-road both, which is why the congregation's trust is the
  real key.

## SECTION 4: THE NAME IN THE GRAVE: THE GRAVE SITE

Locked (doc 36 ruling 7): her peasant name is learnable only where
no one would look: carved on an unmarked grave; the girl who became
the witch died the night of the Fall (the name is buried with her);
the player learns it in the neutral line by finding the grave she
made for herself under another name; saying her name aloud in the
final confrontation is the one line of dialogue that breaks her
composure. Doc 36 open question 6: grave biome, whose ground, what
guards it, light/dark-line echoes. This section authors that
placement (PROPOSED throughout).

### 4.1 Biome placement (PROPOSED)

The grave sits in the MOOR MARCH (doc 03's moor biome, south-west of
the New Capital's farmland band), at the eaves where the moor meets
old imperial farmland: THE SECOND GRAVE, an unmarked stone in a
line of shepherd-markers on a rise above a drover's track.

Reasoning: the neutral line is walked by players who are of neither
court (doc 12: neutral plays the good clock, outlawed in neither
realm's open war). The moor is the one biome a neutral player
crosses without court escort, between the light farmland and the
wild interior. Doc 08 lines 84-89: the Moor March scar is one of
the three blight scars; the grave is placed at the scar's far
western eave, OUTSIDE the blight line, in living moor: the witch
buried her girl-self at the edge of the wound, not in it
(PROPOSED reading of doc 36 ruling 7's grammar).

### 4.2 Whose ground (PROPOSED)

The grave is on NO ONE'S ground: old imperial church-ground,
abolished, unclaimed by any hold (doc 13's territory layer never
grants the rise). The drover's track is common ground by imperial
survival law (PROPOSED; no doc claims it). This is why an unmarked
grave survived the Fall's aftermath: no house owns the rise, no
court patrols it, no hold's register lists it.

### 4.3 What guards it (PROPOSED, within doc 41 encounter grammar)

Nothing guards it on purpose; the moor guards it by being the moor
(doc 41's wilderness encounter grammar: moor threats, weather,
Pale Tide fronts waking wild strains per doc 08's Pale Tide note).
The only deliberate guardianship (PROPOSED): the grave is under a
PALE TIDE WARD, a moon-fog binding she set herself the night of
the Fall; during a Pale Tide front (doc 08 lines 112-119: locked
storm grammar) the rise is unreachable, fog-locked, and any player
camped on the rise is moved out by the fog without damage. The
world itself closes the door one night in a season.

### 4.4 Light/dark-line echoes (doc 36 open question 6 placement)

A LIGHT player can find the grave and not understand it (doc 36,
open). Grammar (PROPOSED):

- The grave's marker is blank to any player who has not walked
  the neutral line to its rumor: an unmarked shepherd stone, one
  among nine, no interaction prompt beyond the examine verb.
- On examine without the neutral line's context: the light player
  reads "another shepherd's marker" and gains nothing. No lore
  codex entry fires (doc 44 Part 2 ledger: no placement beat
  reveals a forbidden fact).
- On examine WITH the neutral line's rumor-tier context: the
  ninth stone reads wrong: shepherd-markers face the track, the
  ninth faces the scar. The name is on the stone's underside,
  readable only by crouching at the grave's foot.
- Dark-line echo (PROPOSED): dark-court players who already hold
  the Veil Spire's shame door (Section 3.2) may reach the scar's
  root and find the witch's OWN record of the night, but the name
  is not there: the name is only ever at the grave (locked, doc
  36: learnable only where no one would look). The witch-authored
  record itself is an open question: WQ-7 (raised in the
  open-questions section).

### 4.5 Rumor-tier path to finding it (doc 41 grammar, PROPOSED)

- Tier 1 (common moor rumor): shepherd-markers on the drover's
  track are older than the shepherds; free at any moor tavern.
- Tier 2 (neutral-line rumor): the ninth stone is counted wrong
  by every shepherd alive; bought from a neutral drover with a
  favor, not coin.
- Tier 3 (door trust rumor): a Shadow Court elder (doc 08 lines
  125-128: the Shadow Court, outlawed in both realms) who walked
  the moor the night of the Fall will walk the player to the rise
  once, if the player carries no court banner and asks in the
  elder's own mother tongue. This is the grave's only guided
  entry; every other finding is stumbling.
