## THE HUMAN CENTER PRINCIPLE (locked 2026-09-12, Nicko)

Character creation stays human-centric (locked doc 03). But ONE HUMAN
can become all the corruption strains across a playthrough: vampire,
undead, werewolf (doc 19/20/21), via their transformation spine lines.
The game is the story of a mortal who can end it as anything.

### Transformation State Rules (PROPOSED for Nicko review)
- ONE STRAIN AT A TIME: the body holds one corruption. Taking a new
  strain requires resolving the old (see switching below).
- Strain states: Human (base) / Vampire / Undead / Werewolf. The
  strain determines: your kit (doc 17 kits, wolf kit doc 21), your
  day/night clock behavior, which courts will take you, and which
  titles read you as what.
- SWITCHING STRAINS: each strain's exit is a death-rite. Vampires
  die in the sun or by stake; undead are laid to rest or unmade; the
  wolf's exit is the Court's unbinding. Switching = complete one
  strain's exit condition, then run the new strain's entry line as a
  corpse or as a mortal depending on the strain's rules. (Vampire to
  undead is the shortest path: the rotted dead rise. Undead to
  vampire requires rising FIRST to living: the Mercy sect's cure,
  doc 19. Proposal: only the Church of Mercy can move a player
  backward to human, which makes Mercy strategically the switchboard
  of the whole transformation web. For review.)
- AXIS GATING (locked from doc 12/17): vampire and undead entry
  lines are deep-evil gated. The wolf line is neutral-keyed (doc 19
  Hidden Court). A deep-good player has NO corruption entry (the
  light court's lines exist to purify, not to turn: their
  transformation is the Sanctified apex, not a strain).
- DEATH PENALTY INTERACTION (locked doc 04 souls drop): your strain
  does not change the death penalty. Undead players DO drop their
  currency and recover it at their corpse. Undeath is not a
  respawn-rule change; it is a body change.

## THE VAMPIRE LINE: THE SUITOR'S SEASON (PROPOSED, rough outline)

Dark court transformation chain. Gated deep-evil; doc 17 patronage
rules apply (any deep-evil player can earn it, no race gate). Texture:
seduction, patronage, the house taking you in on purpose. The vampire
courts convert the living to fill ranks (doc 17 rank-filling), but a
DELIBERATE turning of a champion is a house investment: they will
court you before they will spend the Embrace.

1. THE NOTICING: a vampire house's steward shadows you after deeds
   the court values (dark standing threshold). The invitation is a
   courtesy: you are asked, not taken.
2. THE SUITOR'S SEASON: a season (N in-game days) in the house's
   service as a living client. Night errands, the house's court
   politics, learning the blood-tithe hierarchy from the underside.
   You may leave freely ONCE (the house notes your flight; a second
   acceptance is not offered on the same terms).
3. THE LEDGER OF GIFTS: the house shows you the price openly: the
   hunger, the sun, the name's slow distortion (doc 14), the debt
   your elder carries (doc 19 debt arc crossover: this is where a
   dark player can first learn the throne's secret). You sign the
   mortal waiver: your human life is the entry payment.
   [RE-POINT 2026-09-14, story audit doc 45 finding D12: the stage-3
   disclosure is an UNKNOWING CLUE, not a discovery door (doc 20's
   one-door rule for chain D stands): the house shows the elder's
   tithe LEDGER but the player reads it UNREAD (a glimpse, not an
   explanation); the debt's understanding still arrives only through
   chain D beat 1-2, which this stage now feeds as its
   transformation-line trigger.]
4. THE EMBRACE: doc 17's The Embrace, administered by the house
   elder while your heart still beats. You die mortal and rise
   vampire. (Mechanical note: the player's first vampire death
   sequence plays the full souls death, then the rising scene.)
5. THE FIRST TITHE: tutorial feeding; the vampire kit (doc 17:
   Blood Tithe, Veil of Mist, The Embrace, Domination Gaze) becomes
   yours, levels by its own use (locked kit model).
6. THE COURT'S WELCOME: presented at the ruler court (doc 12 dark
   throne); the house name question (adopt the house per doc 14's
   embrace-as-adoption tradition, or stay unhouse: unhouse vampires
   keep freedom, lose the house's standing perks).

Endings within the line: the willing turn (full court standing), the
REFUSED turn (walk away at stage 3: the house keeps your signed
ledger page as leverage, a grudge), and the FORCED turn (if you die
to a house's Embrace while hostile: you rise a thrall, a darker state
with the retinue-slave rules, doc 11; escapable per doc 19's Veil of
Mist escapes).

## THE UNDEAD LINE: THE NECRO-ARRANGEMENT (PROPOSED, rough outline)

Dark court transformation chain. Gated deep-evil. Texture: the
opposite of the vampire's seduction: the undead court is FORMAL,
CONTRACTUAL, BUREAUCRATIC. You do not get seduced into undeath; you
make your arrangements, pay the fees, and die properly. The
necro-aristocracy is a legal system (doc 14: status = age of death +
deeds since).

1. THE ROT'S BARGAIN: approach a necro-aristocrat house (undead court
   standing threshold). Unlike the vampire house, they hear your
   request without flattering you. A clerk takes your details.
2. THE ARRANGEMENTS: the paperwork chain, and it is a quest chain:
   your WILL (who inherits what you drop on death: dark humor, the
   souls drop goes to your chosen heir), your GRAVE (site choice
   matters: soil, crypt, charnel-land: doc 14 conquered-hold charnel
   lands), your GRAVE-GOODS (what you are buried with is what you
   rise with: the loot system made personal, doc 06), and your
   DEATH-TITLE (doc 14: you draft your own death-name; it can be
   redrawn later as deeds accumulate).
3. THE DOSE: you take Grave Rot willingly (doc 17's T1 spell,
   administered by the house's rot-caster or self-cast at
   threshold). You are now living-on-borrowed: a visible decay
   window (gameplay debuffs, the court's physicians tend you). The
   window is the line's timer: die within it.
4. THE DEATH: any death while rotted works. Battle, accident, the
   rot itself. Per the corruption mechanic (doc 17): those who die
   rotted RISE. Your rising is your rebirth; the death penalty drop
   still happens (locked doc 04: undeath changes the body, not the
   souls rules), recoverable at the corpse you rose FROM.
5. THE RISING: you wake in your grave-goods as the house's newest
   death, death-age zero, lowest necro-status (doc 14: everyone
   above you has been dead longer). Your title is the draft you
   filed.
6. THE FIRST DEED: the house assigns your first deed-since-death
   (tutorial for the undead kit, doc 17: Grave Rot, Miasma of Decay,
   Wail of Despair, Corpse Bloom; locked kit model, four abilities).

Design contrast with the wolf trial: the wolf Court tests RESTRAINT
(hunt alone, keep the human face); the necro-court tests
PREPARATION (die well, plan your rising, pay your fees). Vampire
house courts CHARM. Three conversion cultures, three quests-textures.

## TRANSFORMATION SWITCHING (PROPOSED, for Nicko ruling)

- Exit vampire: the Church of Mercy's cure (doc 19 schism: their
  holiest work, requires a Mercy standing threshold and rare
  components) returns you to human, or death by Aegis steel (the
  light court's answer: Aegis-killed vampires do not rise, doc 17
  locked Aegis rule; Aegis death is final for the strain but
  returns the player to human, the mercy of finality).
- Exit undead: the laying-to-rest quest (your own grave rite,
  self-performed with a Purify-grade rite: doc 17's Purify cleanses
  another's taint, and the Mercy doctrine extension: the WARDENS
  perform it on willing undead, parked proposal). Death by holy
  ground = final rest, back to human.
- Exit wolf: the Hidden Court's unbinding (the reverse of the
  trial's last page) or death by silver while under the Court's
  law.
- Rule: exits always route through a death-shaped event (the
  strains are deaths you keep walking from); switching strains
  means dying into the next line. A player can be vampire in one
  act and undead in another: the body only holds one, and the
  lines' patrons compete for your corpse.

## THE GREAT CITIES AND THE SIEGE TRIGGERS (LOCKED 2026-09-12, Nicko)

legion-breed proposal above):
- PRE-FALL MAIN CONFLICT: the world of men, elves, and dwarfs was
  fighting the ORC SCOURGE. Orcs were a nearly mindless race of
  brutes, an EARLY CREATION OF THE DEVIL HIMSELF, made to conquer
  the mortal world. (They predate the pact: the Undersovran's first
  hand on the world, older than the Fall, the proof of what the
  thing beyond the Veil wanted all along.)
  [RETCON 2026-09-14, Nicko, story audit doc 45 finding D3: orcs
  predate the Fall but NOT the witch's pact. The "Devil's early
  creation" origin is IN-WORLD MYTH the witch manufactured or
  absorbed into the debt's paperwork: the orc city's old-borderland
  religion is her history. Doc 36's "she invented him" stands
  absolute; the demons are the debt's enforcement agents, not a
  pre-witch god's host. Orcs still predate the Fall as a race; only
  the creditor's authorship is reattributed.]
- POST-FALL: orcs naturally sided with the dark court, and now have
  their own rulers under the elf-vampire court. Since the Fall orcs
  have become MORE SOPHISTICATED: they now build their own towns,
  trade, and cities. The Fall's end of constant holy war is what
  gave them space to become a people; their brutality is their
  origin, their civilization is their newness. (Doc 14's
  earned-leadership culture stands: it is the scourge's command
  structure maturing into law.)

THE FOUR GREAT CITIES (locked):
- DARK COURT:
  - MINOR CITY: the ORC CITY (orc-built, the scourge's first true
    city, their proof of becoming).
  - CAPITAL: THE VEIL SPIRE (working name): the PREVIOUS SOLAR
    THRONE, where the elf-vampire king and queen rule. The old
    capital of the whole world, held by the corrupted line since
    the Fall; its sun-motifs survive under the Veil's stains.
- LIGHT COURT:
  - MINOR CITY: the UNDERGROUND DWARF CITY (forge-city, doc 14
    dwarven hall-depth = status made capital-grade).
  - CAPITAL: the NEW CAPITAL (working name): where the human king
    and queen rule; the light court's crown built after the Sunder.

THE SIEGE QUESTS (locked, Nicko): when playing either side, a MAJOR
spine quest is THE SIEGE OF THE ENEMY'S MINOR CITY (the orc city for
a light player, the underground dwarf city for a dark player). THE
CONQUERING OF THIS CITY IS WHAT TRIGGERS THE COMEBACK EVENT (doc 13)
for the losing side in the mid-to-late game:
- Light player takes the orc city -> the dark court's debt default
  begins (doc 19: the demon invasion, doc 13's mass demon ritual).
- Dark player takes the dwarf city -> the light court's comeback
  event fires (doc 13's divine prayer event).
Design consequence: the comeback event is no longer only
debt-default-driven (doc 19's Undertow fuse); it is now
SIEGE-TRIGGERED via the minor-city conquest, mid-late game, on both
sides. The debt fuse remains the dark court's lore truth; the siege
quest is the mechanical trigger for both courts' comeback events.
Cross-check doc 13: the comeback-event scope item now has its
trigger condition (minor-city siege).

## THE WORLD BEFORE THE FALL (PROPOSED sketch, revised with the orc
ruling above; for discussion with Nicko; not locked)

The pre-Fall state answers what each race WAS when there was one
crown (doc 19: the Solar Throne, sole ruler of the continent), with
the pre-Fall MAIN CONFLICT being the realm's war against the orc
scourge. (The "Devil's early creation" framing in the ruling above
is IN-WORLD MYTH per the 2026-09-14 retcon, story audit D3; see the
bracket there.)

- THE AGE OF THE SUN: the elven Solar Throne reigned; the three
  altars (Sun, Veil, Deep, doc 19) kept ceremonial balance. DEATH
  WAS FINAL: no undead existed. NO VAMPIRES existed. The world's
  dangers were the ORC SCOURGE (origin reattributed 2026-09-14,
  story audit D3: the "Devil's early creation" doctrine is the
  witch's manufactured myth; the scourge itself is real and
  pre-Fall), plus
  natural dangers (beasts, the wild wolf strain, war).
- ELVES: the throne's race: court, priesthood, and altar-wardens.
  The Fall split the elven race into the Veil lines (who drank the
  pact's gift: vampires, doc 14's name-distortion) and the
  dawn-refuser lines (the light court's sylvan/high elves, doc 14).
  The elven civil war IS the war's oldest wound.
- HUMANS: the mortal provinces' backbone: stewards, soldiers,
  record-keepers of the realm. Post-Fall they inherited the light
  throne and built its NEW CAPITAL (locked above). Texture
  proposal: humans' short lives are why the light court reads as
  urgent and zealous; a human generation is a long elven
  afternoon, and the light court is building its legacy in a hurry.
- DWARVES: contract clans (doc 14), the mountains' forge-cities.
  PROPOSAL: the dwarven masons BUILT the three altars' stones in
  the age before elves, which is why the Deep Altar (doc 19) still
  stands in the underworld: dwarven masonry outlives its kingdoms,
  and the underworld's neutral folk have been unknowingly keeping
  a dwarven foundation warm. Post-Fall: their UNDERGROUND CITY
  (locked above) is the light court's minor city, the forge-city
  that anchors the light court's war industry.
- ORCS: REVISED (Nicko ruling above) then RETCONNED 2026-09-14
  (story audit D3): the "Devil's early creation" origin is in-world
  myth the witch manufactured or absorbed (see the bracket in the
  Great Cities section); the race itself is real, pre-Fall, and the
  pre-Fall main enemy of men, elves, and dwarfs. Post-Fall they
  sided with the dark court and have become more sophisticated:
  their own towns, trade, cities (the orc city, locked above),
  rulers under the elf-vampire court. Doc 14 stands with the
  reading: their earned-leadership culture is the scourge's
  command structure maturing into law.
- UNDEAD: did not exist before the Fall. The first undead were the
  Split's dead: death itself tore when the pact burned through
  (doc 19), and the era's dead stopped resting. The necro-
  aristocracy is the INSTITUTION the undead built afterward: dead
  houses continuing themselves (doc 14).
- WEREWOLVES: origin question, three options (IO proposals, for
  discussion): (a) the pact's overspill: corruption that slipped
  the ledger and lodged in the wilds; (b) the DEEP ALTAR'S WARDENS:
  the altar's old guardian strain, corrupted by the world's wound
  rather than the pact, gone feral when the altar broke, the Hidden
  Court being the re-disciplined remnant who still unknowingly guard
  its approaches (ties the Signer's ace to the Third Altar path:
  the wolf den IS the altar's threshold); (c) older than everything,
  the moon's own strain. IO lean: (b), because it makes the Hidden
  Court load-bearing for the true ending.
- THE COVENS: PROPOSAL: the Old Faith predates the Solar Throne
  (moon-and-veil keepers, the Veil Altar's first tenders). The pact
  perverted their altar: the Old Faith schismed into the Veil
  Faithful (who welcomed the pact) and the purists (who never
  forgave the throne for using their altar). Post-Fall, "Old Faith"
  reads as the dark court's coven religion (doc 12) but its oldest
  doctrine is ANGER AT THE PACT, which is the Old Blood sect's
  root (doc 19).

## Resolutions (2026-09-12, Nicko, second pass)
- Transformation switching: RESOLVED: death-shaped exits only, no
  strain stacking (doc 19).
- Backward path: RESOLVED: NO backward path, strains are one-way
  until death; the Mercy-switchboard proposal is SUPERSEDED (doc
  19). Mercy's cure work stays NPC-facing (Purify another's taint).
- Werewolf origin: RESOLVED: option B, the Deep Altar's wardens
  (doc 19); the wolf den IS the altar's threshold.
- Wolf player clock: RESOLVED: INVERTED wolf clock (doc 19).
- Strain re-take after cure: RESOLVED by the no-backward-path
  ruling: since no cure exists, a strain is exited only by dying
  into the next line, so re-taking the SAME strain means dying back
  into it, which is allowed but costs the exit event each time.
- Orc origin: RESOLVED (Great Cities section above) then
  RETCONNED 2026-09-14 (Nicko, story audit D3, bracket in the Great
  Cities section): the "Devil's early creation" origin is IN-WORLD
  MYTH the witch manufactured or absorbed; orcs predate the Fall as
  a race but NOT the pact. See the RETCON bracket there; this
  resolution line survives as provenance.
