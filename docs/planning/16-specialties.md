# 16 - Specialties: Titles and Bonuses

Proposed 2026-09-11 by Nicko (loves the layer; wants MORE combinations with
titles and bonuses; example build: an undead-aligned neutral GRAVEROBBER).

## The Specialties System
Specialties are COMBINATION UNLOCKS: earn titles by pairing skill
thresholds, and sometimes affinity/standing conditions. Each specialty:
- Has a TITLE (displayed under the character's name; NPCs can react to it).
- Requires a SKILL COMBO (2-3 lines at threshold levels).
- May require an AFFINITY or COURT STANDING condition (axis position,
  faction standing, day/night behavior).
- Grants BONUSES (passives, unlockable abilities, discounts, unique
  interactions).
- Design intent: every specialty is a build IDENTITY, not just a perk. A
  player who sees "Undead-Allied Neutral Graverobber" knows exactly what
  that character does. Titles are roleplay anchors and build shorthand.

## The Example Build (Nicko's)
UNDEAD NEUTRAL GRAVEROBBER:
- Skills: Stealth + Lockpicking + Hunting/Skinning (or Herbalism for grave
  herbs) at thresholds.
- Stance: NEUTRAL on the axis (has survived the Allegiance Gambit) with
  UNDEAD FACTION STANDING (friendly to the dark court's undead houses -
  trade access, no attacks unless provoked).
- Bonuses (proposal): undead creatures ignore you unless provoked; grave
  goods worth more; cemetery maps/keys drop more often; Dark Pacts spells
  learnable at neutral (undead patrons teach them); necro-aristocracy
  retinue recruits available.
- Flavor: you rob graves with permission from the dead's own court.

## Rules (first pass)
- Specialties are EARNED, not chosen: meet thresholds -> title awarded
  (with a small in-world ceremony or recognition moment where sensible).
- LOCKED 2026-09-11: titles appear on the character screen (alongside the
  moral axis) AND NPCs REACT TO THEM IN DIALOGUE. The reactions are the
  payoff: a Graverobber gets different tavern rumors, a Witchbane gets
  Church contracts offered unprompted, a Six-Times-Refuser gets wary
  respect from both courts' envoys.
- Multiple specialties can stack (a character can be Graverobber AND
  Black-Marketeer AND Six-Times-Refuser) - the title list grows with the
  build.
- Affinity-conditioned specialties (3, 6) create court-locked builds;
  neutral-conditioned ones (10, 12) reward the gambit survivors.
- Bonuses are tuned so no specialty is strictly "must-have": they express
  playstyle, they don't gate power (fairness floor).

## Locked Decisions (2026-09-11, second sweep)
- Specialty stacking: 2-4 specialties max at endgame. Titles stay
  meaningful and readable; unlimited stacking would make the identity
  layer noise.
- Content gates: MOSTLY bonuses, but a small set of APEX SPECIALTIES gate
  exclusive questlines - deep specialization unlocks a named quest chain
  (Nicko's example: deep necromancy specialization unlocks the LICH KING
  questline - journey to become a lich, a super-powerful magic user and
  summoner). APEX SPECIALTIES to be designed for good, bad, AND neutral
  (one or more apex chains per stance).
- LAPTABLE SPECIALTIES: LOCKED 2026-09-11. Titles with AFFINITY/STANDING
  conditions lapse when their conditions break (a Witchbane who drifts
  from deep good loses it; a Graverobber who swears allegiance loses it);
  titles recording HISTORY (Six-Times-Refuser) or pure SKILL thresholds
  (Fry Cook) are permanent. Lapsable titles are re-earnable by restoring
  conditions.
- UI REQUIREMENT (locked 2026-09-11): the in-game character menu must
  CLEARLY SHOW each active specialty's RETENTION CONDITIONS - what must
  stay true to keep the title, and what breaks it. Players plan
  specialty transitions: they can see which conditions to keep, break
  deliberately, and re-earn, moving between specialties over a
  playthrough. Condition transparency is the design contract; the title
  system is legible by design.
- The lapsability examples (Witchbane, Corpse-Smith, Graverobber,
  Six-Times-Refuser, Fry Cook) live below in the Examples section.

## Lapability Examples (IO, for review)
"Lapsable" means the title checks its own conditions continuously and
disappears if they break. Worked examples:
1. WITCHBANE requires deep-good standing. If the player's axis drifts to
   neutral or evil (massacres, dark deeds), the Church no longer
   recognizes them: the title and its anti-witch bonuses lapse. Re-earn
   by returning to deep good.
2. CORPSE-SMITH requires dark court standing. If the player betrays the
   dark court (aligned-court betrayal is near-permanent enmity per doc
   12), the undead smiths revoke their patronage: title lapses.
3. GRAVEROBBER (neutral build): if the player SWEARS allegiance to either
   court, they are no longer neutral - the title lapses (they can earn
   court-flavored titles instead).
4. THE SIX-TIMES-REFUSER: this one is naturally PERMANENT - the six
   denials happened and cannot be undone; it is a historical fact about
   the character, not a living condition.
5. FRY COOK: pure skill-threshold specialty, no standing condition, so it
   NEVER lapses.
Reading: titles with AFFINITY/STANDING conditions are lapsable; titles
that record HISTORY (Six-Times-Refuser) or pure SKILL thresholds (Fry
Cook) are permanent. A lapsable title re-earnable by restoring its
conditions.
