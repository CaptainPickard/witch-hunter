## HOW TO READ THIS DOC

This doc AUTHORS the beats that docs 20/21/44 define structurally. It
changes no structure, no mechanic, no ruling. Every beat carries the
ruling it rides. Beat format (used throughout):

  BEAT NAME. Location: ... NPCs: ... Verbs: ... Pays/Costs: ...
  Failure: ... Rides: (citation)

- NO NEW LORE: every beat derives from cited doc text. Where a beat
  needed a fact no doc provides, it is logged as an OPEN QUESTION with
  an assignment (doc 08 format), in the open questions section at the
  end of this doc.
- PROPOSED NUMBERS appear only where this pass was asked to provide
  them: door standing thresholds and beat counts. All are marked
  PROPOSED and are GDD-tunable.
- IDENTITY RULES (doc 44, non-negotiable, respected in all beat text):
  the Signer is the WITCH's mask; the Pale Queen is the separate
  neutral vampire who leads the brokers; the light queen is Queen
  Maren of the Dawn (the witch); doc 36 pre-correction "Pale Queen"
  references mean the WITCH. No beat re-litigates this.
- KNOWLEDGE LEDGER (doc 44 Part 2) is the reveal source of truth: no
  beat lets a character learn a fact their ledger cell forbids. Reveal
  beats carry a LEDGER: line checking the table.
- House style: no em dashes anywhere; terse GDD prose, not prose
  fiction.
