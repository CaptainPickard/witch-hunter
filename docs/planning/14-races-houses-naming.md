# 14 - Races, Houses, and the Conquest Chain of Command

Proposed 2026-09-11 by Nicko. Procedural nobility generation rules: race-flavored
naming and house traditions for every race, plus the conquest chain of command
(who leads, who wins, who assumes control).

## Conquest Chain of Command (locked with Nicko)
- CLAN LIFECYCLES STAY WITH THE CONQUEST LAYER: houses rise and fall ONLY
  through conquest outcomes, not through separate background simulation.
- Before each conquest, the faction LEADER (king/queen of that court)
  CHOOSES certain houses or families to lead the conquest of a territory.
- When the battle is won or lost, the VICTOR CHOSEN BY THE KING is the one
  who assumes control of the territory.
- Implications: the crown's favor is a resource; houses compete for the
  king's choice; a losing house's fortunes collapse with the defeat. The
  king's selection is a political act the player can influence (standing
  with the court, prior service, retinue strength).
- Design intent: conquest leadership selection makes each victory a
  political event, not just a military one. The map moves AND the peerage
  reshuffles with each battle.

## Naming Conventions by Race
The procedural nobility generator is RACE-FLAVORED: each race has its own
naming grammar, house-name style, titles, and traditions. Names must read as
authentically belonging to their race and culture.

### HUMANS (Light Court; the Free Cities and human kingdoms)
- Personal names: late-medieval European register. Male: Aldric, Bertram,
  Cedric, Godfrey, Rowan, Tommaso. Female: Elsbeth, Isolde, Mireille,
  Rosalind, Yvaine.
- House names: two styles - toponymic (de Hallowmere, von Ashford,
  of Blackmoor) and descriptive/occupational (Ironhand, Goldwarden,
  Stagborne).
- Titles: King/Queen, Duke/Duchess, Count/Countess, Baron, Sir/Knight.
- Tradition hooks: chivalric oaths, hereditary knighthoods, cadet branches
  (second sons found new minor houses), tournament culture, house words/mottos.
- Conquest flavor: victorious houses receive a deed and a new banner; the
  king's chosen gets the hold and the tax right.

### ELVES (Light-aligned sylvan/high elves; ALSO the dark throne's race)
- Personal names: flowing, vowel-rich, consonant-paired. Male: Aerendil,
  Caladwen, Finvaris, Thalorin, Yllavan. Female: Aurethil, Elandria,
  Nimriel, Silmara, Vaelith.
- House names: nature-coupled, often two-word (Silvermoon, Dawnmere,
  Elderbough, Nightbloom) - and the DARK throne's elf-vampire houses skew
  darker: Duskmantle, Veylthorn, Nightshroud, Ashveil.
- Titles: the light courts use High King/High Queen, Lord/Lady of the
  Glade, Warden; the dark elf-vampire court uses King/Queen, Prince/Princess
  of the Veil, Blood Count.
- Tradition hooks: extremely long memory (elves never forget a slight - old
  feuds outlive houses), bloodline purity obsession (darker for the vampire
  court: pureblood lines matter), trees/stars/blood as heraldic motifs.
- Conquest flavor: elven houses are few but old; losing an elven house is
  losing centuries of accumulated power. The vampire court's houses gain
  power by drinking from defeated rivals (literal and political).

### DWARVES (Light/neutral-leaning; forge-city clans)
- Personal names: hard consonants, clipped. Male: Balin, Durgin, Krom,
  Thargrim, Vondrek. Female: Berylla, Dagna, Helga, Marda.
- Clan names: compound craft-geography words: Stonefist, Emberforge,
  Deepdelver, Goldbeard, Ironhallow.
- Titles: Clan Lord, Forgefather/Mother, Under-King, Thane.
- Tradition hooks: clan = guild + family (craft monopolies), rune-recorded
  lineages, grudge keeping (dwarves never forget debts OR slights), hall
  depth = status.
- Conquest flavor: dwarves conquer by CONTRACT: they sell siegecraft to
  both courts and claim mining rights in conquered territory rather than
  the land itself.

### ORCS (Dark court; tribal-horde structure)
- Personal names: guttural, forceful. Male: Ghrashk, Morguk, Skarn,
  Urzoth, Zulrag. Female: Ashka, Ghorza, Naghra, Yevka.
- Clan names: war-earned, not inherited: Skulltaker, Bloodfang, Ironmaw,
  Wraithbreaker. New clan names are TAKEN when a warband does something
  legendary.
- Titles: Warlord, Warchief, Blood Chief, the Horde's voice.
- Tradition hooks: no hereditary landed nobility - leadership is EARNED in
  battle and challenges; a warlord who loses is challenged; clans absorb
  the defeated rather than exile them.
- Conquest flavor: orcs don't receive land grants in the noble sense -
  territory is HELD by whoever beat the last chief. The king's "choice" is
  really the right to be challenged next.

### UNDEAD (Dark court; necro-aristocracy)
- Personal names: their OLD name in life, plus a death-title. Malachar the
  Hollow, Dame Isolde of the Drowned Crypt, the Unsleeping Warden.
- House names: the family line persists past death: House Mordrake,
  Lineage of the Pale, the Evermourne.
- Titles: Dread Lord, Bone Regent, the Sepulcher's Voice.
- Tradition hooks: necro-aristocracy - status = age of one's death + deeds
  since; houses are "continued" by their undead founders, so an undead
  house NEVER fully dies while its bones stand.
- Conquest flavor: conquered human holds become tributary charnel-lands;
  the dead of the conquered are levied.

### VAMPIRES (Dark court; the elf-vampire throne's peerage)
- Personal names: elven base names, corrupted by the Veil: Aerendil ->
  Aerenith, Silmara -> Sylmordh. The longer un-dead, the more the name
  distorts.
- House names: Veylthorn, Duskmantle, Umbravine, the Crimson Court houses.
- Titles: Blood Count, Veil Duchess, the Thorned Prince.
- Tradition hooks: the embrace (turning) as adoption into a house;
  blood-tithe hierarchy; elder-younger peerage (a "younger" house can
  eclipse its elder through conquest favor).
- Conquest flavor: vampire houses prize HUNTING RIGHTS and blood tithes
  over territory paper-title; a hold conquered by a vampire house still
  shows its old banner... at night.

### DEMONIC (Dark court; bound-patron houses, rare)
- Personal names: true names are hidden; mortals know them by epithet: the
  Brass Whisper, Ashmother, He-of-the-Ninth-Wound.
- House structure: not houses at all - PACTS. A "house" is a circle of
  mortals bound to one demon patron; leadership is the patron's favor.
- Titles: Patron, the Bound, Whispersworn.
- Conquest flavor: demonic "conquest" is possession: the territory's
  ruling line gets replaced with a bound mortal (the patron's chosen).

## Cross-Race Neutral Houses
The neutral court's mercenary companies and smuggler crews also have
house/clan-like structures, race-mixed:
- Mercenary company names: functional and boastful (the Broken Shield
  Company, the Ninefold Blades, Graywatch).
- Smuggler/underworld rings use stolen or ironic noble styling (the House
  of Closed Doors, the Velvet Chain).

## Generator Rules (first pass)
1. Each race has: name pools (male/female), house/clan name grammar,
   title ladder, heraldry motif set, one or two tradition hooks.
2. Houses are created ONLY at conquest-grant moments (chain of command
   above): the king picks a house, the house gains the hold.
3. A house's race comes from the faction that conquered (a vampire-court
   conquest can grant a hold to a newly-turned human knight, etc.).
4. Defeated houses: fade (dissolve into the population), flee (reappear
   later as claimants), or bend the knee (join the new court's peerage).
5. The player can enter this system: earn a land grant, found a minor
   house, or have a retinue member elevated.

## Marriage, Race-Shift, and the Player's House (locked with Nicko)
- HOUSES CAN CHANGE RACE OVER GENERATIONS, PROCEDURALLY, through the
  MARRIAGE SYSTEM: inter-race marriages merge house traditions, and the
  child line's identity drifts over generations (a human house that marries
  into an elf line slowly becomes an elven house in name and custom).
- THE PLAYER'S OWN FOUNDED HOUSE: the player chooses the name, and it is
  THE SAME FIRST AND LAST NAME chosen at character creation at game start.
  The player's house is the player's name; founding a clan is writing
  yourself into the world's lore.
- LORE-CHANGING RIGHTS: the player gains increasing rights to change the
  world's lore as their political standing rises - all the way down to
  BANNER CREATION.
- BANNER CREATION GATE: unlocked when the player wins their FIRST conquest
  territory capture and is granted a hold. At that moment the player can
  create their house banner (and, by extension, design the heraldry their
  clan will be known by).
